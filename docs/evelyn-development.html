Development status / October 2026

A demonstration being built—not a finished download.

The current development reports describe Evelyn as a separate Qwen-based AI and technology historian in progress. A historical Kira conversation test harness has been located for adaptation, but Evelyn's original voice greeting has now been recorded and approved. Her public installer, spontaneous model-backed dialogue using this voice, synchronized lip movement, visitor-memory persistence and repeated live-conversation tests remain unverified. A separate controlled eyelid blink has passed.

October 19 development update: Visitor-specific application-memory namespaces and reusable detailed head controls are being added. A frozen 14-turn test with three synthetic visitors is prepared to check delayed recall, restart persistence, visitor separation, unknown visitors and a corrected preference. The test is not yet executed. Live recall, audio, geometry and clean installation remain unverified; saved application memory is not proof of neural learning. Documented limits ↗

October 20 head-preview milestone: A newer local compatibility-render test now shows Evelyn’s licensed 3D head candidate from distinct front, three-quarter, and side angles. Developer screenshots report that all three visible saved views passed blank-screen checks and matched their direct canvas captures. The preview uses modified diffuse materials and reduced textures; the original PBR appearance remains unqualified. The controlled eyelid test is now verified; speaking, synchronized lip movement, generated conversation, visitor memory after restart, and a public installer remain unverified. Evelyn Hart and Avatar Builder · October 23, 2026 · Independently reviewed source

Her body, library and original voice move closer to testing.
Source review is not a completed demonstration.

Three new engineering steps passed their described independent and primary source-code reviews. None supplies new live conversation audio, a naturally moving full body, or an installer ready for public download.

01 / BODY AUTHORING

163-bone export checks

A checked design now maps 25 joint chains to a canonical 163-bone rig and 139 skin-weight groups. The export auditor compares the package, build report, vertex reference, GLB, Blend and rig-rest files. It verifies declared joint/hierarchy, rest-transform and sampled skin data—not natural deformation, full anatomy, realistic clothes or physical motion. The new export execution gates remain disabled.

02 / DOCUMENT LIBRARY

Document-import fixes reviewed

The app now has reviewed source fixes for preserving a selected library destination and a supplied document title/date while recursive discovery is in progress. The staged ordinary-file library remains 30 publications plus two original AI/film-technology primers. These are files in preparation, not material Evelyn has actually read. Installation, import, page-reading and restart behavior still need runtime tests.

03 / ORIGINAL VOICE

Audition and recording adapter prepared

A new source-reviewed adapter retains Evelyn's original adult female voice design, 55-word audition text, profile and seed. The planned test records before generation and plays the generated voice through admitted physical speakers while retaining receipts. The staged app's 28-file mapping was reviewed. No unique voice has yet been generated, played, recorded or approved.

Not yet established: human-audible speech, live visitor conversations, full internal anatomy, accepted natural movement, fabric motion, installer operation, or an exported body copied and verified in Patient Horizon. Failed and partial future recordings remain part of the planned review. Private books, reference media and visitor memories are not published.
