Bounded trained-on result

Public2: 0/2 → 1/2

After 64 updates and 128 presentations of repeated examples, NewBrain correctly output the single-token target ball after saving and reloading in a fresh process. The three-token target cup word one instead ended immediately without an answer.

This is one trained-on example, not held-out learning, broad language ability or delayed retention.

Negative learning result

Original12: 2/12 → 0/12

A separate frozen 12-query experiment used 64 updates and 384 repeated presentations. The two baseline passes were missing-information responses—not learned historical facts. Following training and reload, neither passed. The original 2/12 state was preserved.

The cause remains unknown. No hidden answer repair or easier benchmark was applied.

Internally independently reviewed sample

Sampled gradients: 30/30

A separate saved-data review independently checked 30 sampled parameter coordinates at each of three fixed step sizes: 90 central differences drawn from 181 original objective calls, across ten parameter arrays containing 11,914 model parameters. All selected comparisons met the declared tolerances.

This verifies a local sample at one Public2 checkpoint and batch, not every gradient, an explanation of the Original12 regression, improved learning, or external academic replication. Earlier failed audits remain documented.

Avatar and sensory engineering

A body, tested
one component at a time.

Geometric movement and invented-voltage controls are useful infrastructure; they are not biological skin, nerves or a closed-loop learning body.

Measured geometry

512 weighted vertices · five poses

A recorded forearm-command study moved all 512 sampled weighted skin vertices at the nonzero pose settings and restored the exact saved coordinates and pose channels.

No proof of passive elasticity, sliding skin, touch sensing or visual realism.

Internal engineering result

8/8 synthetic event controls

One fixed synthetic skin-event component passed eight predefined criteria, including zero/nonzero inputs, duplicate suppression, two-avatar isolation, session/epoch rejection and state/reset behavior. The nonzero invented input produced 0.00025 V versus 0 V for the zero control.

Units and inputs are invented engineering values, not calibrated biological signals.
