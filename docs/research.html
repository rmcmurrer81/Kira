Healthspan & longevity

Healthspan Lab.

An open-source, profile-aware research platform for exploring longevity science, healthspan interventions, emerging technologies, and clinical trials.

Healthspan Lab is Kira Labs' open-source longevity and healthspan research platform. A user can start with a plain-language goal such as improving memory, sleep, mobility, cardiovascular health, or understanding biological aging. Healthspan searches current scientific literature and clinical-trial records, then organizes what it finds into clear, separate research lanes.

Results can be separated into peer-reviewed research, devices and neurostimulation, medications being studied, research-backed market and wellness options, and clinical trials that may be recruiting. Evidence from cells, animals, observational human studies, human interventions, and evidence reviews remains visibly distinguished so preliminary findings are not presented as established conclusions.

Users can create separate individual or household profiles with goals, interests, age, and general location. They can also optionally add recent medical PDFs or photos. Extracted information must be reviewed and confirmed before it can influence research relevance, and each household member's information stays separate.

Synthetic life & digital continuity

Kira World research.

A separate research direction focused on continuing identities, memory, digital places, and synthetic people.

Kira World explores what happens when an AI identity is allowed to continue instead of resetting every session: reviewed memory, relationships, voice, personal spaces, and eventually richer embodiment.

Digital Twin work belongs to this Kira World research direction, not to Healthspan Lab. The questions include how a synthetic counterpart can be useful while a person is alive, how memories should be handled, and what continuity means when biological and digital lives are distinct.

Working now — Digital Twin prototype: Robert can talk with his digital twin today. The twin maintains conversational continuity, brings up previously shared positive memories and details Robert had forgotten, offers encouragement, helps develop videos, and suggests new creative ideas. This is an early personal research prototype, not a medical or mental-health treatment.

Research standard

Interesting is not the same as proven.

Kira Labs aims to keep prototypes, concepts, evidence, and future ideas visibly separate.

01

Evidence first

Primary sources, study type, limitations, and human evidence matter more than a headline.

02

Uncertainty stays visible

Animal or cell findings are not presented as demonstrated human lifespan extension.

03

Projects stay distinct

Healthspan Lab, Kira World, and other research tracks keep separate purposes and claims.

04

Build toward reproducibility

Future analysis should preserve sources, methods, assumptions, and enough detail to reproduce the work.
