Information Reliance Before Information Readiness

BUSINESS REALITY

Information continuously flows across enterprise systems and workflows to inform and drive outcomes.

OPERATIONAL REALITY

Organizations can rely upon information even when its accuracy, completeness, and context have not been established.

THE MISSING CONTROL

Organizations lack a dedicated, systematic control mechanism to determine the readiness of upstream source information BEFORE downstream reliance.

03 / Consequences: Systems Information Reliance

Systems
Information
Reliance

When organizations rely on information BEFORE Systems Information Readiness is established, business consequences can become human consequences.

INFORMATION BECOMES

BUSINESS CONSEQUENCES

Can include:

  • Erroneous approvals or denials
  • False fraud or risk flags
  • Customer remediation costs
  • Manual rework and delays
  • Regulatory penalties and audit findings
  • Reputational damage and loss of trust

HUMAN REALITY

Can include:

  • Loss of access to credit, insurance, or benefits
  • Delayed medical care or essential services
  • Lost employment and advancement opportunities
  • Financial hardship and economic instability
  • Disproportionate harm to individuals and communities
  • Erosion of public confidence and trust
