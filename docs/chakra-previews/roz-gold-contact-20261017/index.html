Information
Readiness Gap

Explore the framework

01 / CURRENT STATE

Information continuously flows across enterprise systems, technologies, and business workflows to inform decisions, trigger actions, and drive business outcomes.

02 / OPERATIONAL REALITY

Organizations rely upon information even when its accuracy, completeness, context, and reliability have not been established.

03 / THE MISSING CONTROL

Organizations lack a systematic control mechanism to determine the readiness of source information before upstream information informs downstream decisions, triggers actions, or drives business outcomes.

04 / BUSINESS CONSEQUENCES

Reliance on information whose readiness has not been established can transform a valuable business asset into a potential liability, creating financial losses, operational disruption, and regulatory exposure.

05 / INFORMATION BECOMES HUMAN REALITY

Business outcomes can determine real-world outcomes—business and intelligent systems actions shape the human experience and the realities of people, workforces, and the communities they serve.
