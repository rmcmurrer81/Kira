# Chakra.io – October 17 seven-panel website review

This responsive, functional webpage is a design proposal based on the visual layout and text in Roz Griffie's supplied `Griffie 7 Panel Website - Saturday 17 October.pptx` (nine PowerPoint slides including supporter strip and footer). Text was manually transcribed from the images in the deck; no claim is made that the proposed products are deployed.

Visual sections: 1. Information Becomes; 2. Systems Information Readiness Gap; 3. Six-part Chakra ecosystem; 4. Who We Are; 5. Four technology areas (ROSA, HCI, Decisioning Intelligence, Category Creation & Education); 6. Intelligence Dimensions; 7. Supported By; 8. Contact; footer.

Product CTAs open a short informational dialog; they do not claim to open operational MVPs. Supporter logos are reproduced from Roz's source ZIP and labeled as in her PowerPoint. Original Kira Labs main website remains untouched. Mobile layout is native HTML rather than screenshot images.
