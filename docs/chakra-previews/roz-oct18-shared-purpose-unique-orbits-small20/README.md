# Chakra full-site review: 20% smaller card words/buttons and four distinct orbital designs

New preview: https://kiralabs.org/chakra-previews/roz-oct18-shared-purpose-unique-orbits-small20/

Source preview: https://kiralabs.org/chakra-previews/roz-oct18-shared-purpose-softened-accents/

Requested changes, limited to the four technology cards:
- The small ROSA/HCI/DI/RIT labels, card headings, descriptions, and Explore buttons are scaled to approximately **80% of their previous sizes** at desktop, tablet, and phone breakpoints. Button width, height and padding are scaled as well.
- The ROSA, HCI, DI and RIT orbital decorations have distinct elliptical proportions, tilts and satellite locations rather than near-identical orbital geometry. Their existing cyan, softened gold (20% more transparent), softened purple (40% more transparent), and silver-white palettes are unchanged.
- The compact desktop two-column section remains intact; at narrower widths the cards still stack.

The complete original website is otherwise unchanged, including the central animated six-node Information Readiness orbit, the 10%-strengthened gold background, earlier typography and copy corrections, working product-dialog buttons, footer and privacy section.

Previous review URLs were not overwritten. This is a nonproduction interactive design concept.
