A source-backed starting point

What Florence could know on 31 July 1890

30 July 1890

A surviving wax-cylinder recording made for veterans of the Charge of the Light Brigade provides direct evidence of her historical English voice. Wellcome has documented two renditions from the same session.

10 South Street, Mayfair

Florence's documented London home, near Park Lane. Letters from 1889 confirm the address; her long periods of poor health and home-based work matter to an accurate reconstruction.

Historical context · London, 1890 compared with the 2020s

Her familiar world would look very different from ours.

This comparison describes the period, not a proven diary of Florence's personal day. Her home circumstances differed greatly from those of working-class Londoners. Dates distinguish what already existed on 31 July 1890 from technology introduced later that year.

TopicLondon as of 31 July 1890Today (2026)
TravelHorse-drawn cabs, omnibuses and trams; steam-powered rail and Underground services. The first deep-level electric Underground line opened to passengers only in December 1890, after the chosen snapshot.Cars, buses, electrified Underground lines, high-speed trains and commercial flight.
Light and powerGas streetlamps and candles or gas in many buildings; electric light was available in a few high-profile places, not most homes. Coal was a major source of urban fuel and smoke.Widely available electric lighting, electronic appliances, central heating and digital infrastructure.
CommunicationLetters, printed newspapers, telegraphs and some telephones, mainly among wealthy households and businesses. The recorded voice was still an unusual medium.Smartphones, email, video calls, global digital publications and instant messaging.
MedicineSanitation, trained nursing, antiseptic procedures and germ theory were progressing, but antibiotics and X-ray diagnosis had not yet arrived.Antibiotics, modern vaccines, imaging such as CT/MRI, advanced surgery and networked clinical records.
Public lifeQueen Victoria's reign, strong class divisions and Britain's global empire. Women still lacked a vote in UK parliamentary elections.Universal adult parliamentary suffrage, a very different legal/social landscape, and instantaneous global communication.

Planned character and learning architecture

Historical knowledge, followed by new experiences

  1. Begin at a dated historical boundary

    Florence starts with a source-grounded account of what she could have known by 31 July 1890. Uncertain details stay explicitly marked as reconstruction. She does not start with knowledge of her later death or twenty-first-century inventions.

  2. Discover a shared library without reading everything

    New books can appear in the planned Documents\Kira Labs\Library location. Discovery reveals a title and catalog details only; unread passages should not appear in her knowledge. Her own reading pace and pauses determine what she encounters.

  3. Form new memories and changing opinions

    New information enters her individual dated reading notes and conversations when she actually encounters it. Learning how she died, for example, would be stored as something read from a later biography—not as a remembered experience of dying.

  4. Adapt expression gradually, without losing provenance

    Modern speech habits and vocabulary could gradually influence her conversational style through repeated interaction. The historical voice reference, reconstructed delivery, source-grounded knowledge and newly formed memories must remain separate and testable.

Independence matters: Evelyn Hart and Florence Nightingale are intended as separate applications, shortcuts, voices, personal histories and memory stores. They may share a book catalog, but neither may automatically inherit the other's reading, private experiences or conclusions. Their interaction, installer and reading gates are not yet proven working.

Avatar Builder · Proposed experiment

Can a dated portrait guide a recognizable 3D head?

Florence provides a useful future controlled Avatar Builder experiment. Build a head and hairstyle from properly dated visual references, keep unknown angles and materials identified as reconstruction, compare rendered front/three-quarter/side views against the references, and then test eyelid and mouth animation as separate acceptance checks.

Not yet validated: portrait-based likeness, Florence's original or synthesized speaking voice, unscripted conversations, her historical knowledge boundary, shared-library discovery, gradual language adaptation and installed Windows shortcuts. A concept, a source collection or a designed desktop icon is not a completed model or an operating program.

Archival references · Verify each source and reuse right

Research trail

  • Wellcome Collection, voice recording first rendition, 30 July 1890 — a historical source, not yet a validated synthetic voice.
  • Wellcome Collection, second rendition from the same recording session — not an independent lifelong voice sample.
  • University of Alabama at Birmingham, Florence Nightingale letter of 29 June 1889 — confirms the South Street address and gives firsthand writing style.
  • Historic England, 10 South Street property history — confirms her London residence.
  • National Portrait Gallery, Florence Nightingale iconography — identifies an 1887 painted portrait and 1889 photographs, with artwork rights to confirm.
  • Project Gutenberg, Notes on Nursing — useful historical writings, with edition and text-date verification before inclusion.

Later compilations, later portraits and modern biographies can be research tools for the developers, but should not silently become facts known to the 1890 character. Book redistribution and voice/image rights are assessed separately from whether an archival page can be viewed online.
