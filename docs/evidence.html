NewBrain / Dated internally reviewed test outcomes

Learning tests, including the failures.

The narrow short-response success is real within its fixed synthetic test conditions. It does not supersede the unchanged main assessment, the five-question regression or the longer-answer failure.

ExperimentObserved resultInterpretation and next question
Original12, latest 256-update study2/12 → 2/12Two newly correct complete answers replaced two previously correct missing-information answers. No net accuracy improvement.
Three-question short teaching plus cold reload2/3 → 3/3 → 3/3Three explicitly rehearsed synthetic cases were correct after training and separate restart. Held-out facts were not demonstrated.
Different longer-answer teaching test2/3 → 2/3First content token changed from b to cab, but the model ended before the required source and version tokens. Full-response acceptance failed.
Five-question pilot with separate reload2/5 → 0/5 → 0/5Baseline correct missing-information responses regressed and remained incorrect after restart. This was a negative result.
Frozen sampled-gradient diagnostic80/80 + 4/4Numerical derivative comparisons passed their fixed limits; maximum absolute difference about 2.08 × 10−9. This is not a learning-score result.

Patient Horizon / Avatar Builder / Work to test next

From geometry and records toward testable anatomy.

What has been recorded

Four synthetic patient request-planner checks and a separate body-engineering demonstration: 512 sampled weighted skin vertices moved through five commanded poses and returned to their reference positions. The restored geometry can be measured; it is not equivalent to validated tissue or organ behavior.

Patient Horizon work status →Measured skin report (PDF) →

What is still a research plan

A versioned synthetic patient record, a transparent anatomical view, numerical breathing reference tests and clinician-reviewed evidence are the proposed lung demonstration milestones. There is no verified live clinical system, patient-specific physiological lung simulation, or treatment-prediction result.
