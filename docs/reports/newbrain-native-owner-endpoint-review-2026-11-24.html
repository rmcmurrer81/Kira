Native still fails exact copying and ownership swaps

Public aggregate review of committed summaries and manifests. The experiments recorded independent cold replays; this publishing review did not rerun model training or release private research assets.

Frozen question endpoint comparison

In 128 fresh synthetic requests, the same saved decoder and learned head were used with the same candidates, scores eligible for final selection, and abstention rule. Only the question-state position changed. There were zero optimizer updates.

The owner-name endpoint abstained on every request. It removed the only correct supported copy and one false copy. The fixed head had been trained with question-end states; this test does not establish what retraining on another state would accomplish. Independent cold replay reproduced raw scores and outputs; all 80 earlier raw outputs matched, 65/80 exact under the historical LF-plus-terminator convention.

Separate source and literal-copy diagnostics

In a distinct forward ownership swap study, unrestricted Native achieved 0/64 exact supported answers, the preserved learned reader 2/64 with correct source, and a deterministic declared reference 64/64. Complete swaps: 0/32, 0/32 and 32/32 respectively. Unsupported abstentions: 57/64 Native with LF then EOS (0/64 adjacent EOS), 44/64 learned, 64/64 declared under the adjacent-EOS convention. Restricted complete-item ranking was 22/64 for the learned reader; it abstained on 17 of these, made three partial/non-item selections and copied two exactly. These are not equivalent output routes.

On a separate marker-swap set, Native copied 0/64 supported values in either ending format and completed 0/32 pairs. Its LF-then-EOS abstentions were 68/96; the declared reference resolved 64/64 supported and 96/96 unsupported requests. A distinct literal-copy test found 0/72 supported answers and 0/72 correct first tokens even with explicit row cues and eight one-field copy prompts. All three studies used zero training updates; their results are not pooled.

Matched first-answer-token training

Two branches trained 64 local GPU updates each with the same batches and replay order. Ordinary and extra-first-token loss improved fitted first-token matches to 6/32 and 10/32 (baseline 0/32), but all three roles produced 0/64 held supported answers and 0/32 complete held swaps. Historical exact LF-ending scores remained 65/80 in each role; both candidates lost three previously correct answers while gaining three others. Both failed the frozen retention and copy gate; cold replays reproduced the outputs.

Deterministic declared lookup and snapshots

A separate opt-in lookup produced 96/96 exact synthetic results: 36/36 supported and 60/60 expected abstentions. All 12 immutable snapshots reopened from two namespaces, with exact source spans and independent process repeatability. The caller supplied the anchor, relation and requested field. No language-model forward or optimizer step ran, so these are deterministic source-access checks, not learned recall, personal memory or natural-language intent recognition.

Limits and status

These are distinct assistant-authored synthetic test banks and different scoring contracts, not a combined benchmark or human-independent evaluation. No new model qualified or was adopted. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. No private code, prompts, individual records, checkpoints or reference assets are released.
