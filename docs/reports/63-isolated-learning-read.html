 How the automated test was made and run
 1. Freeze the question, controls and scoring
 The study used one fixed public synthetic association and two questions requiring an answer that information
 was unavailable. Before training, the association had to be incorrect and both controls correct. Those
 eligibility checks passed. The expected responses and evaluation rules were fixed before the run.

 Generation was scored as a complete response, including its required answer, source/version fields and
 ending. The evaluator did not repair answers, choose a more favorable checkpoint or replace failed cases.
 This was a test of taught examples, not a held-out generalization benchmark.

 2. Run one declared training schedule
 One training process used the native all-parameter update path for 64 updates. The record contains 12
 explicit objective evaluations, 64 objective evaluations inside the update routine and 12 generated responses
 across the training and zero-update control measurements. The zero-update control did not receive equal
 training compute.

 All parallel development paused for the test, cleanup and evidence closure. The 8 GiB available-memory
 startup guard and 3 GiB reserve remained unchanged. The original checkpoint and failed originals were
 preserved; this new state was not adopted as the default model.

 3. Verify saved originals without retraining
 The first saved-evidence audit stopped before scoring because matching large integer file-identity values
 appeared as integers in one receipt and decimal strings in another. That failed attempt remains consumed
 and preserved.

 A narrow checker correction received separate source review. Thirty-five pure metadata controls then
 passed, including exact values above the usual floating-point integer boundary, malformed-value rejection
 and rejection of genuinely changed identities. These controls validate metadata handling, not learning.

 A fresh saved-only audit read the existing run's originals and accepted its scoped process, file, counter and
 response evidence. It did not repeat training. The audit checked complete byte lengths and hashes, original
 reference joins, stream completion, closures and the declared state counters.

 Verification limits: internal role separation is not external peer review. The audit did not independently reconstruct the model, prove
 the complete optimizer correct, establish continuous RAM/CPU behavior or qualify every opaque API-argument ownership detail.

 What is established, and what comes next
 Verified progress
 The sampled numerical-gradient study passed 80 coordinate and four directional comparisons across ten
 parameter groups, while rejecting two deliberately wrong-gradient controls. The latest evidence-checker
 controls passed 35 comparisons. The native training run and its saved evidence can now be examined as a
 documented negative learning observation.

 These are useful engineering and diagnostic results. They do not substitute for a newly correct answer, a
 higher Original12 score or a functioning clinical simulation.

 What the result does not explain yet
 The new association remained incorrect after teaching, even though the recorded parameter hashes
 changed. The present result does not establish whether the limiting factor is the actual update
 implementation, the distribution of training objectives, representation or selection of the answer, or response
 generation.

 A gradient check concerns a sampled derivative of a specified loss. It does not by itself establish that the live
 optimizer applies the intended update to every array, or that reducing that loss improves a strict
 complete-answer score. More RAM or a larger GPU would support experiments but would not guarantee a
 learning fix.

 Next bounded measurements
 The primary next diagnostic is being prepared to compare one actual optimizer step with independently
 calculated expected parameter changes and counter changes, including deliberately incorrect controls.
 Objective weighting and first-token versus later-token errors also require separate examination. These
 follow-ups are prepared work, not completed tests.

 A distinct-process cold restore of the accepted experimental state remains pending. It must use a fresh test
 admission and preserve the negative training observation. The main Original12 assessment and the Qwen
 comparison also require separately defined, verified runs.

 How a university could help
 The most useful immediate support is machine-learning expertise in optimization, sequence objectives and
 experimental design; independent replication; research assistants to run fixed tests; and stable laboratory
 computing access. Additional memory, GPU capacity and remote server access would improve access to
 reproducible comparisons and larger experiments.
