Competitive abstention did not qualify for adoption.

A matched selector treatment added only one correct unsupported abstention over the trained control while losing seven correct grounded answers. The frozen promotion gate failed; no checkpoint was adopted. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

Verified training scope

The unchanged parent selector was restored at step 1,024. The control and competitive treatment each made 256 actual local RTX 5060 Ti CUDA selector-only optimizer updates, to step 1,280 (512 combined), using identical 2,048 training draws. Independent readback checked all 28 terminal tensor storages per arm, optimizer counters and random state. The Native backbone had zero new training updates. This is separate from other November 22 selector trials.

Cold replay and Native retention controls

Independent 384-case static and 128-case supplemental cold replays repeated saved raw tokens, scores, offsets and real first-answer histories for all three selector roles, including incorrect outcomes. A separate unchanged Native backbone check reproduced all 80/80 previous outputs, preserving the 65/80 prior correct replies. Repetition is not newly established learning.

Failed qualification, boundaries and privacy

The treatment's 33/224 correct supported answers were seven below the control's 40/224, for just one additional correct unsupported abstention (61/160 versus 60/160). Eligible source ranking regressed, and supported two-turn conversations stayed 0/32 in all roles. No promotion, no live routing change and no Native retraining occurred. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.
