Original connected neutral body: rendered, tested and rejected

Native geometry and export passes do not equal acceptable appearance: the owner rejected the rendered original connected body, including its face, and rejected subsequent revisions. No person or physiology was activated.

Connected outer-skin candidate

One originally authored neutral outer-skin source connects head, neck, torso, arms, five-digit hands, pelvis, legs, feet and five toes on each foot. It has two declared orbital openings, normalized skin weights and 68 rest bones; its source contains 45,528 vertices, 46,504 faces and 90,744 triangles. Structural provenance, topology and budget checks passed; native whole-body rendering, editable save/reopen and GLB reload subsequently passed. The actual face and body failed visual review and must not be used as an approved Evelyn body.

A second, head-cropped curved-pelvis/upper-thigh revision passed native rendering and BLEND/GLB reload at 30,196 vertices, 30,674 faces and 60,262 triangles, but was also visually rejected. A third parametric revision was rejected and exposed loss of very small skin weights in the strict official exporter; parity criteria were not relaxed to disguise the failure. A new implicit regional-tissue approach and separate original facial surface-net revision are source-ready only: full extraction and native appearance acceptance remain unrun. Independent partial shoulder experiment

A separate partial connected torso-and-arms experiment passed a native scene save and one-mesh GLB import at 16,244 vertices and 32,144 triangles. For the combined measured pose only, the edge-length ratio interval narrowed from 0.497–1.885 with earlier weights to 0.725–1.194 with changed weights. Failed earlier attempts remain failures. These measurements do not establish natural anatomy, exported animation, collision proof or accepted deformation. Regional verification ↗.

Appearance and anatomy limits

No approved Evelyn or separate fictional-character face, anatomically accepted full body, natural movement, trained motion, functioning digestive/urinary/reproductive organs, clinical physiology, or validated body-family generalization exists from these tests. Hair, realistic anatomical openings and contact/deformation remain unqualified. No third-party body/rig was imported as original, and no private likeness, personal reference image, raw animation or operation capture is published.
