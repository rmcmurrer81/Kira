 Longer-answer learning diagnostic
 2/3 → 2/3
 Complete answers before and after training · Separate three-question test

 The latest controlled experiment improved the first answer token on one synthetic example, but did not produce
 the complete required answer. The two explicitly rehearsed missing-information responses stayed correct. The
 no-training control stayed at 2/3. The predefined full-response success criterion failed.

   Positive example                                       Generated answer

   Before training                                        b + end marker

   After training                                         cab + end marker

   Required complete response                             cab word one + end marker



 In this diagnostic, “cab” is the answer token, “word” represents its source and “one” its version. These are
 synthetic research labels. Generating only “cab” earns no complete-answer credit.

 What improved, and what remains unresolved
 The model changed the wrong first token to the correct one. Its teacher-forced whole-answer loss on that
 example fell from 9.692280 to 2.136134. However, it generated an end marker before the source and version.
 Lower training loss and a correct first token are useful observations, but neither establishes a complete learned
 response.

 How this relates to the positive restart result
 The earlier, shorter-answer diagnostic remains a verified positive result: 2/3 before training, 3/3 afterward and
 3/3 after a distinct fresh-process restart. Its no-training control stayed at 2/3. All three rows were explicitly
 rehearsed. Report 68 explains that study. The longer-response experiment here is separate and does not
 replace its result.

 Main Original12 benchmark: still 2/12. Qwen comparison: untested. The latest experiment did not rerun the
 main benchmark, establish generalization, or demonstrate an unseen real-world fact.

 Method, evidence and next work
 How this test was built and performed
 Three public synthetic queries and their complete answer sequences were fixed before the outcomes. Eligibility
 required the positive example to be incorrect initially and both missing-information checks to be correct. All
 eligibility checks passed, and no examples were resampled.

 The trained arm received 64 gradient updates, 192 example presentations, 1,152 prefix tokens and 512 target
 tokens including end markers. The batch normalization denominator was 8. Both missing-information examples
 were explicitly taught. The separate no-training arm received no updates.

 The procedure recorded 12 explicit whole-answer loss evaluations and 12 free generations across arms and
 assessment points. Training also made 64 internal update-objective evaluations. Complete-answer scoring
 required all expected tokens and the correct ending; a constant answer could not satisfy the joint criterion.

 Independent verification
 A separate saved-data audit checked the retained training outputs without making a new model or training call.
 It verified the original evidence references, exported file-I/O accounting, complete output streams, recorded
 ownership and cleanup. The experiment and all selected resource allowances were closed, preserving used
 and unused costs and all failed originals.

 The audit verified 107 native evidence cells, 253 copied references, 219 events, 19 canonical once-close operations, four complete
 output streams and two empty jobs. These checks establish scoped execution evidence; they do not establish continuous RAM/CPU
 measurement, complete optimizer correctness or a diagnosis of the learning failure.

 Decision and next investigation
 The full-response criterion failed, so the proposed cold confirmation remains unrun and unfunded. The failed
 bridge does not authorize another exploratory bridge or automatic progression to the twelve-question
 experiment.

 The next work is analysis of the existing answer, source, version and ending evidence, together with static
 review of target construction and decoding. Early stopping is the observed failure. A specific implementation
 defect, memory-readout problem or forgetting mechanism has not been established.

 Computing resources
 The run completed on the current machine with the existing 8 GiB available-RAM startup guard, 3 GiB reserve
 guard and two-CPU limit. More RAM, GPU capacity and laboratory support can enable larger reproducible
 studies and concurrent work; they do not guarantee a learning improvement.
