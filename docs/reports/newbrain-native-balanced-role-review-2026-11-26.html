Balanced name-role learning recovers one-word names but increases false detections

Completed matched training

A matched local CUDA FP32 comparison completed 72 updates per arm. At the fixed zero diagnostic cutoff, exact held-out complete names rose from 0/96 to 24/96, all one-word names (0/39 to 24/39). Two-word names remained 0/33 and three-word names 0/24 in both arms. No calibration cutoff met the frozen gates.

Held-out no-name clauses with a false acceptance rose from 0/36 to 27/36; false-positive spans rose from 0 to 110. The balanced arm missed all 57 held-out last-word labels and all 57 fit last-word labels, indicating an acquisition failure as well as held-out failure. Two independent read-only restores exactly reproduced all six candidate and role streams.

Only the auxiliary role weighting changed; the frozen fit order and step-zero state were shared. Held-out marked-rival one-word wins increased from 21/39 to 27/39; two- and three-word wins remained zero. Both variants failed the frozen name-selection gate.

Numerical qualification, not behavioral qualification

A separate negative-role mask GPU qualification matched all 5,092 ordinary and 768 negative scores to a scalar oracle across two independent FP32 runs; 36 no-name gradient checks matched exactly. This verifies numerical behavior, not name selection or learning.

Fresh preflight, no training

A fresh synthetic panel comprises 96 fit, 64 calibration and 96 held-out clauses; all 256 round-tripped through the production tokenizer. A fit-only rehearsal wrote its receipt but the CLI exited with a serialization error; an independent report-only check verified the receipt. Zero training updates completed in the fresh phase; no training run was admitted.
