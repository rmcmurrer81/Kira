What was and was not verified

Software validation recorded 118 tests run (84 passed, 34 skipped), with 12/12 packaged trainer and 15/15 evaluator checks. Separate bridge optimizer mechanics had passed seven synthetic transitions and 47 rejection observations. Saved-artifact reviews found no covered inconsistency and fresh-process restoration reproduced even the erroneous replies. These are bounded engineering/persistence checks, not neural-learning success. The reviews were AI author/domain-aware, not human-independent, and all scored study cases are now consumed. The two curricula changed multiple factors together, so no causal explanation of the failure was isolated.
