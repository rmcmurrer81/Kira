Verified selector training and separate replay

Typed, detached-top and detached-approved isolated selector heads each completed 256 actual local RTX 5060 Ti FP32 CUDA optimizer updates from the same preserved step-256 starting state to step 512, using identical ordered 2,048 synthetic case draws. Independent readback and terminal checks passed. These 768 combined updates applied only to small selector heads, not Native language-model training.

Nine local replay jobs (calibration, held-out evaluation and cold-process replay for each arm) completed 384 selector forwards apiece, 3,456 total, with exact output repetition including mistakes. No optimizer updates, Native forwards or paid compute occurred during replay. This report reviews saved research summaries rather than rerunning private GPU experiments.

Unsupported and qualification failures

On 160 unsupported evaluation endpoints, detached-top correctly abstained 128/160 times; detached-approved only 109/160, losing 19 previously correct abstentions and adding 32 newly wrong sourced replies. Calibration floors and the anchor-only gate failed. Distinct calibration absent/conflict/underspecified correct refusals were typed 32/32,32/32,16/16 each direction, and approved forward 23/32,24/32,11/16 versus reverse 12/32,11/32,12/16.

A prior Native retention record was rechecked, not newly run. A subsequent offline rank audit stopped during calibration; its repaired adapter passed 21 fabricated checks but no new diagnostic rate was published.

Review boundaries and privacy

No selector was adopted or activated and Original12 was not rerun. Only aggregate synthetic figures are public, not privately saved code, checkpoints, raw prompts, person data, copyrighted/private character imagery or unlicensed assets.
