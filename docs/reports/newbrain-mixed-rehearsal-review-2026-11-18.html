Execution and audit receipts

The preserved experimental state continued for exactly 2,048 local CUDA updates. A fresh-process cold restore repeated 208/208 raw answers byte-for-byte, including errors, and repeated the state. The saved-artifact audit recorded 116,733 checks over 624 records. Repository CI recorded 118 core tests: 84 pass, 34 optional skips; nine additional combined-qualifier fixtures and source checks were saved. These checks do not measure conversational reliability or generalization.

Methods, negative gates and next steps

This was a single-arm, assistant-authored synthetic before/after experiment; no matched no-replay causal arm or human-independent assessment was executed. Replayed examples were consumed in training; 32 novel phrases refer to old teaching facts, and 96 new contexts use familiar pair labels. All 208 evaluation cases are now consumed. Review did not independently rerun tokenizer encoding, Adam or backend numerical training; sampler/CPU/CUDA RNG was intentionally reseeded rather than replayed from parent state.

The next planned evidence would require separately frozen unseen contexts, positive fact-order copying and new-wording tests plus a matched control. This run does not qualify durable lifelong memory, actual-answer multi-turn conversations, live people, emotion, physiology, Qwen replacement or a trained 9B model. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. The experimental checkpoint was preserved but not adopted; no cloud training session or public model deployment occurred.
