NewBrain can copy a supplied year, but its saved readers did not recognize year questions

This public review compares five newly archived project-authored experiments. The website review checked committed saved summaries, not raw GPU execution. The experiments use assistant-authored synthetic fact records; their counts are not independent human evaluations. Reproducibility of errors does not establish learned understanding. No checkpoint was adopted.

Latest: read-only optional-year test

Two previously trained, saved question readers were evaluated on 512 fresh synthetic questions each with zero training updates. The 64 matched groups preserved question wording while changing whether a year was available, absent or replaced.

Both readers labeled all 64 present-year questions as requests for a person, including their unchanged-question removal and replacement variants. Each reader misreturned a year on 16/64 county questions. The held gate failed. Their earlier 1,536 fit and 4,032 retention outputs and the current outputs were reproduced exactly after a cold restart; this is reliability of reproduction, not correctness.

The supplied-intent fixture is not a learned reader

A separate structural fixture, given the correct field, relationship and anchor directly, passed all 512/512 exact source cases. It copied a present year 64/64, returned unknown when removed 64/64, copied a replacement 64/64, and passed the other exact evidence controls. Its candidate readback confirmed 6,016 complete spans, including 1,920 years. The fixture ran zero model forwards and zero optimizer updates. It tests evidence selection and output transport only; the saved readers failed the learned field-decision task.

Earlier matched question-reader training and negative controls

Two question-only 33,417-parameter heads each completed 192 GPU updates on 1,536 fit examples. On one 768-case held set, the factorized reader correctly answered 72/384 supported questions versus 96/384 for the four-field reader; correct abstentions were 284/384 and 280/384. Both failed qualification, and the four-field head lost 795 previously correct reference answers in 4,032 retention cases.

A later controlled relationship-wording fit changed only the relationship phrase in half of its 1,536 training rows and ran 192 GPU updates. Against its saved control, supported answers improved from 96/384 to 144/384, but correct abstentions fell from 288/384 to 256/384 and unavailable-property abstentions from 72/128 to 28/128. It also failed adoption criteria. These denominators belong to a separate held set and must not be combined with the earlier 768 cases.

A further zero-update split of the saved readers' role and field scores scored 144/384 supported, 284/384 correct abstentions, and 16/128 wrong copies after support removal versus 12/128 for its saved four-field control. It lost 184 previously correct answers in the 4,032-case retention set. The split failed. A consumed wording replay showed strong sensitivity to familiar phrasing, but was not fresh qualification.

Next experimental boundary

Future work can train and qualify requested-field identification and relationship selection separately on truly fresh questions, and retest absent evidence without false copying. An upcoming training plan is not itself an executed or accepted result.

Public evidence and limits

The original decoder and live NewBrain route were not retrained or replaced in the latest read-only fixture and reader tests. No learned real-world memory, natural conversation, multi-turn reasoning or complete embodied person is demonstrated here. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. Source research archives, private question cases, model artifacts, image references and other non-public materials are not republished.
