NewBrain NB15 and NB16: two tested transfer ideas fail

These are separate 8,343-parameter synthetic reference studies, not the accepted NewBrain checkpoint. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, with the prior trained 0/12 failure retained. Positive NB14 value weighting is preserved as a different study; cross-study protocols and worlds must not be pooled.

NB15 · New-question form teaching

Direct-only versus varied-form teaching matched the foundation, architecture, value-token weighting, optimizer, old replay, training updates and presentation budgets. The NEW third question form was reserved outside teaching. NEW alternate questions were taught in the varied arm, so they were not the main held-out measure.

Varied-minus-direct primary transfer was −2/24, with zero positive worlds out of six. The fixed support gate failed. Across 36,864 updates and 294,912 examples, 18 saved states reproduced all 432 generated answers after separate-process reload. Nine original and nine restarted answers were incorrect immediate-EOS empty third-form outputs. Independent saved-evidence review was not independent retraining.
