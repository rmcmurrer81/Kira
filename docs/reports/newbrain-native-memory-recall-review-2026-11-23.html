Saved facts survived restart, but Native did not recall them

Reviewed committed aggregate summaries and manifests, not a new research run by the website reviewer.

Exact storage, failed direct answers

The application saved and cold-restored eight synthetic facts across two isolated profiles without state crossing. Native direct generation from the restored facts scored 0/30 exact, including 0/12 supported questions. Raw before/after replies matched on 7/8 paired questions. The decoder emitted unknown plus newline 23 times, empty output four times and an unrelated name three times. Ten of 12 supported questions produced the newline abstention. All 30 outputs reproduced exactly in a cold process. Zero model updates; decoder, optimizer, counters, randomness and history unchanged. The strict byte, token and termination scorer was not relaxed.

Read-only likelihood diagnostic on consumed prompts

Two separate local GPU processes each scored 120 fixed continuations across 20 prompts in eight forward passes; raw likelihoods matched byte-for-byte. The stored answer ranked above unknown in 0/8 matched post-write cases, both bare and with a linefeed before EOS. Its first token also ranked above unknown in 0/8. The relative likelihood improved in 4/8 bare and 2/8 linefeed cases, but no stored answer beat abstention. Unknown plus linefeed outranked bare unknown in 20/20 contexts. Removing a newline alone would not resolve the recall failure. Zero optimizer updates; restricted continuation ranking is not an independent new behavior benchmark.

Separate trained qualifier scope comparison

A 12,387-parameter derivative matched the control's initial weights and optimizer state, 256 development questions and 64 GPU group updates. Exact scope classification improved from 80/160 to 90/160 on fresh questions; all 87 previously correct date controls were retained. Required dates 8/40 to 11/40, required sources 7/40 to 8/40, incidental mentions correctly kept plain 65/80 to 71/80. Nine incidental mentions became false requirements; the frozen scope gate failed. No answer evaluation or original reader/decoder training occurred in this study.

Limits and next test

No checkpoint adopted, no evidence of general recall or open conversation. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. The proposed next visible-fact copy and abstention test with fixed retention remains a plan, not a result. No private inputs, source assets, histories or checkpoints are published.
