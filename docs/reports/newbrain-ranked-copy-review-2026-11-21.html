Ranking failed to improve fact binding.

A further familiarity comparison did not restore reliable swapped-fact lookup. An experimental source-copy path now passes bounded engineering checks, not a trained or qualified model improvement. Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

Separate executed work

Two matched training arms began from an unchanged step-22,800 full-state checkpoint on the local RTX 5060 Ti in deterministic FP32. Each ran the same 512 causal batches, and ended at step 23,312. The treatment added only ranking forwards/objective; the standard arm was the causal control. Both cold-restored trained checkpoints reproduced raw generations and saved state. A later lexical diagnostic used zero optimizer updates. Synthetic cases do not establish human-language learning.

Separate inference-only lexical transfer follow-up

On baseline, standard and ranked checkpoints: exact supported generation was 4/384, 1/384, 1/384; both answers right after ownership swap 0/192 all; correct missing-fact abstention 34/48, 47/48, 46/48; false abstention despite support 269/384, 364/384, 358/384. Earlier correct controls stayed 65/80, 49/80, 52/80. Familiar words did not reliably recover changed relationships. Reused synthetic labels and template prompts limit generalization.

Separate source-copy mechanics, not learned selection

A read-only RTX 5060 Ti observer produced byte-identical ordinary and observed decoder logits, features and greedily generated tokens on 3/3 development prompts; 103/103 candidate spans emitted exactly. The original candidate domain covered requested literals in only 1/3 prompts. Later pinned CPU-tokenizer checks, after preserving and repairing a failed whitespace-boundary case, emitted 627/627 enumerated candidates and 12/12 requested source occurrences exactly. A proposed separate selector passed 8 synthetic PyTorch CPU checks and 9 provenance checks, without loading NewBrain weights or running training. Correct bytes do not prove that the decoder can select the right source answer.

Qualification and baseline

No checkpoint or selector was promoted; ranked learning and retention failed, lexical transfer remained weak, and the proposed source selection was not fitted or evaluated on model accuracy. The preceding inference-only ownership diagnostics and inverse-render negative training are separate archived studies. Accepted Original12 2/12, not rerun.

Next work is planned, not completed

Freezing a separate training split, fitting a provenance/abstention selector, and testing complete swaps, unsupported prompts, retention and cold restarts remain research proposals. No measured neural answer gain or production deployment is established.
