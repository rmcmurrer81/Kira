Matched token-embedding readout ranks all supported candidates, but support errors persist

Public aggregate review of committed summaries and manifests; website review did not rerun the GPU experiments or inspect the private binary evidence archives.

Held comparison — ranking gains, qualification failure

In a new 608-case synthetic comparison, a token-embedding interaction ranked 320/320 supported candidates correctly versus 255/320 for the matched contextual interaction. Exact supported answers rose from 74/320 to 176/320 and correct abstentions from 205/288 to 231/288. The treatment lost 13 previously correct supported answers and 27 correct abstentions, so it failed qualification. No checkpoint adopted; accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

The treatment recovered 115 supported answers and lost 13; it recovered 53 correct abstentions and lost 27. It still emitted 57 false copies on unsupported cases. The first scoring preflight failed on a fabricated unknown option before inference; the corrected retry passed eight fabricated checks without changing the scoring rule.

Separate matched fit and reproduction

Matched auxiliary readouts completed 120 CUDA updates each on the same 456 fit cases, with zero Native decoder updates. Independent cold restoration reproduced all 1,216 calibration logits across both readouts. Two held passes reproduced raw evidence and decisions byte-for-byte; held inference used zero optimizer updates. The only changed input channel was contextual versus pooled token-embedding interaction; four other channels matched exactly. Each held pass processed 76 Native batches, 1,824 forced options and 12,730 target tokens through EOS. No free-form generations ran. The original model state was preserved.

Saved-score support attribution

All 144 remaining supported misses had the correct first-ranked candidate but two support scores above 0.5. In 136/144 misses the extra positive was a mismatched ownership record; 8/144 involved borrowing or storage. High-overlap ownership mismatches produced 243/484 false positives versus 0/28 low-overlap. The association does not establish causality. All 608 saved decisions and 688 archived contrast pairs were authenticated by the diagnostic. Six diagnostic checks and 15 fabricated weighting tests passed. An initial diagnostic assertion conflated outside-only ownership with outside matches alongside eligible answers; the saved retry separated those groups without changing original scores or gates.

Prepared follow-up, not a model result

A fresh support-only comparison bank has 456 fit, 152 calibration and 608 held cases. Exact tokenizer admission and independent serialized tape readback were recorded; the proposed weighted support comparison, feature capture, training, retention replay and behavioral scores remain open. The next comparison keeps copy scores fixed and tests extra weight on high-overlap ownership records whose lookup differs. Neither new training nor held evaluation has been performed in the reviewed bank record.

Limits

Supplied candidate and query spans on assistant-authored synthetic records are not unrestricted dialogue or proof of neural memory. Earlier 1,152 ranker cases, 80 Native drift cases and eight fallback controls remain unmeasured for these readouts. No model adopted; accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.
