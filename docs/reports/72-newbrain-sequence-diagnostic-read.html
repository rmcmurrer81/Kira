 Where the longer answer fails
 A completed read-only diagnostic localized two weak decisions in a separate three-case teaching exercise. It did not
 train the model, change a checkpoint, or rerun the larger assessment.


    Original12                                            New training updates                      Qwen comparison

 What was measured

    Decision inspected                                                          Required symbol               Model's choice


    Immediately after the correct first answer word                             Next word                     End marker
                                                                                4.38% probability             4.77% probability


    At the next position, with the correct preceding word                       Following word                A different word
    supplied                                                                    3.62% probability             16.97% probability

 Supplying correct preceding words is an inspection technique, often called teacher forcing. It reveals a decision at that history; it does not
 establish that the model generated the whole history unaided.

 What this tells us
 The first suffix decision narrowly favors ending the answer. A later word decision is also incorrect even with the
 correct preceding word supplied. Simply preventing an early end would therefore leave another demonstrated
 sequence error.

 The two missing-information controls still strongly favor their expected tokens at the fixed diagnostic positions. These
 observations narrow the next learning experiment without proving a particular implementation defect.

 What remains unresolved
 This is a three-case diagnostic, separate from Original12. It does not by itself explain every error in the larger
 12-case study, establish generalization, or demonstrate a new learning gain. The saved negative experiments and
 the original baseline remain preserved.

 Next step
 Prepare one separately defined teaching-order comparison at the same dose, with a preserved baseline, an
 untreated control, both missing-information guards, and a fresh-process reload after a joint pass. The stopped failed
 experiment will not receive further updates. This next experiment is preparation, not a successful result.

NewBrain sequence-learning diagnostic                                                                                         October 20, 2026




 How the test was built and checked
 Scope: one preserved failed state; three fixed synthetic examples; eight fixed answer positions. One example has a
 longer structured answer and two are missing-information controls.

 1. Freeze the inputs and plan
 The checkpoint, protocol metadata, source versions, fixed positions and limits were pinned before execution. The
 diagnostic used the preserved failed state as read-only input. Training, free generation and new model-state products
 were prohibited.

 2. Run once with current gates
 Parallel tracks were held. Current thread, paused reminder, source closure and fresh test receipts were checked.
 Available RAM was 16.8 GiB before the run, above the unchanged 8 GiB startup guard; the 3 GiB reserve guard and
 existing process limits remained in force.

 3. Inspect the decisions without further teaching
 One native objective batch produced eight fixed gold-history readouts. The complete loss and output probabilities,
 logits and output-bias gradients were retained for independent reconstruction. No result-based retry was used.

 4. Recompute the arithmetic independently

    Check                                                             Verified result


    Normalized loss                                                   1.0836942996357317; reconstruction difference 0


    Output-bias gradient                                              All 42 coordinates checked; maximum absolute difference 1.39e-17


    First suffix logit margin                                         Required word minus end marker: -0.0854257

 The first suffix margin equals a shared output-bias difference of -0.6886893 plus a residual of +0.6032636 from all other logit terms. This
 is descriptive algebra, not proof that output bias caused the failure. The check does not newly validate all recurrent-gradient coordinates.

 5. Audit originals, cleanup and costs
 The independent saved-evidence review checked 219 native events, 106 raw argument cells by inverse hash, 19
 canonical once-closes, four complete owner streams and two empty process Jobs. Available original file-I/O
 evidence was closed. Full selected allowances, including unused portions, were permanently closed.

 The audit used saved evidence only: no new model, provider or native-execution calls. Source-exported I/O counters are not complete
 operating-system-wide totals. Continuous RAM/CPU behavior and a causal defect remain unproven.

 Evidence: consumed execution 1050 and independent saved-evidence audit 1051. Public methods and current status. Complete private
 originals and failed checkpoints are retained.
