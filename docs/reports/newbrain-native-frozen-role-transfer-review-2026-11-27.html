Frozen role features fit familiar cues but fail held transfer

Completed matched trials

Two separate matched 60-update CUDA trials failed qualification. In the equal-norm comparison, held supported clauses were 2/84 in both trained arms (unchanged checkpoint 1/84), correct empty clauses 36/36 in both, and natural-style supported clauses 0/24 in both. Correct cue direction rose from 12/36 to 18/36, but both signs were correct in 0/36 pairs.

The earlier matched readout trial produced 2/84 versus 0/84 held exact supported clauses for the existing versus paired objective; held true-name ranking was 9/84 versus 10/84 and false clipped spans fell from four to two. Both failed the older name-retention veto (0/2). Independent cold processes reproduced the saved output streams.

Both equal-norm arms used the same saved step-72 checkpoint, 480-clause replay and 60-update schedule; encoder optimizer slots stayed at clock 72 and the role slots reached 132. Two separate cold processes reproduced all 15 raw output blocks.

Saved-score diagnosis

A read-only scalar audit of 744 saved synthetic score packets showed that in either equal-norm arm, a wrong candidate tied or outranked the correct span in 76/84 held supported cases. Only three correct spans ranked strictly first but remained nonpositive; threshold adjustment alone cannot recover the outranked cases. Earlier exact controls fell from 24/24 to 22/24 in both arms.

Independent scalar reconstruction matched all recorded zero-cutoff span selections. This audit performed zero model forwards or optimizer updates.

Frozen contextual-feature probe

A separate frozen contextual-feature probe used two independent CUDA processes, 448 label-free forwards each, with identical raw features and logits. A fit-only affine probe scored 36/36 familiar pairs with both signs correct versus 0/36 for the original head; it scored 0/24 calibration and 0/36 held pairs. Held relative direction fell from 24/36 for the original head to 6/36 for the probe. No probe was inserted into the model and no original optimizer update ran. The probe's failure does not show that the encoder lacks all recoverable contextual information.

The original scorer on this distinct probe fixture achieved 0/84 exact held supported clauses, 36/36 held absent clauses correctly empty and 0/24 supported ordinary-prose clauses. The original checkpoint, optimizer state and training history were preserved. The failed transfer is a property of this bounded probe and evaluation, not proof of absence of contextual information.
