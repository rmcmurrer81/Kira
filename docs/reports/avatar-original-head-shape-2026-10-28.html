The final explicitly projected neutral geometry round-tripped exactly in the saved evidence, but an intermediate raw symmetry failure remains documented. A finite, successful geometry audit does not establish that the facial anatomy or proportions are realistic.

The visible defects remain

The head still has a bulbous cranium, elongated and insufficiently deep facial plane, unclear mandibular profile and a visibly unnatural submental/skull-to-neck transition. A predominantly lateral widening adjustment cannot fix a front-to-back depth deficit. The next candidate should use the original B-spline shape controls to adjust the cranium, forehead and midface depth and mandibular angle, with fixed-camera front and profile references.

The neck attachment metadata and 128 original boundary positions were preserved. Seven female-torso calls were not run. No accepted individual head, fitted eye, mouth, expression rig, original body, organ simulation, motion or medical anatomy is demonstrated. The previously accepted standalone eyeball component remains separate from this failed full-head fitting exercise.

Research boundary

Private identity recipes, raw renders, source photos and desktop records stay private. The 25,214-frame desktop custody capture is not a public avatar video or proof of rendered animation. No copied ready-made head mesh was substituted. The saved report and measurements are an internal reviewed source, not an externally replicated scientific publication.
