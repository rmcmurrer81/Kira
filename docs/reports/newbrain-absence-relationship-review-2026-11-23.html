Improved copying still failed abstention and retention

Three source-committed research summaries are reviewed here. These are saved local results, not research re-executed by the website reviewer. An executed GPU comparison, a zero-update intervention and an untrained component fixture are separate forms of evidence and must not be pooled. No reader or checkpoint qualified.

1. Completed training and retention check

Four isolated residuals ran 64 local GPU optimizer updates each (256 total). Each reader's held set contained 384 supported and 384 unsupported assistant-authored synthetic questions. Earlier retention checked 4,032 stored replies. The underlying Native model and decoder were not retrained.

The replay-only absence penalty gained eight supported correct answers and four abstentions relative to its field-only comparison, but still lost 327 previously correct retention replies. A cold process reproduced outputs exactly, including wrong ones; reproducibility does not establish qualified learning.

2. Supplied-relationship diagnostic, not learned recognition

On a separately consumed 768-case synthetic diagnostic, supplying the correct relationship to saved scoring data recovered 136/198 wrong copies from the two absence-trained readers and 96 supported answers per reader. The balanced absence reader moved from 216/384 to 312/384 supported exact and 304/384 to 368/384 correct abstentions under intervention. The replay absence reader moved from 144/384 to 240/384 supported and 266/384 to 338/384 abstentions. These are not independent model predictions: the relationship was provided to the checker.

All 4,608 saved decisions reproduced exactly, with zero model forwards, tokenizer calls or optimizer updates. Supplying both authored indices to a deterministic source rule yielded 384/384 supported and 384/384 unsupported correct labels, but cannot be counted as model question understanding.

3. Relationship-score fixture, source-tested only

An isolated correction preserved all four requested-field scores while changing relationship scores. Seven fabricated component checks passed on CPU, and the same 7/7 passed on the local RTX 5060 Ti; restart and synthetic next optimizer state matched exactly. Preparation produced 448 synthetic fit cases and 768 held cases, with paired wording and evidence interventions. This stage ran zero project-model forwards and zero project optimizer updates. Actual model training, exact-token readback and independent retention qualification remain pending.

Public scope and limits

The experiments use assistant-authored synthetic data. Improvements in one held score do not establish natural dialogue, robust fact retrieval, or learning across settings. The wrong-copy errors also involve requested-field selection. No candidate was adopted; the original decoder was unchanged; accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. Private source archives, case tapes, model artifacts and unapproved imagery are not reproduced.
