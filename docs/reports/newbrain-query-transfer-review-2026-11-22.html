Query-switch training and fresh transfer remain unqualified

Two separately source-committed internal studies are summarized here using aggregate data only. The website review checked their saved summaries and did not rerun GPU experiments. Cases were assistant-authored synthetic records, not independent human evaluation. No model was adopted.

The 458/4,032 and 407/4,032 prior retention losses were carried forward; they were not rerun in the transfer study.

A correct candidate rank did not guarantee an answer

In a separate wording audit, the paired reader ranked the correct copy first on both wordings in 121/192 supported cases, yet turned an exact answer into unknown in 48 cases. In 46 of those, the correct copy still ranked first. Mean correct-copy logit declined 0.642, abstention logit increased 0.047 and the margin narrowed 0.689. Distinct failures affect abstention calibration and direction transfer.

A source-only parser fixture generated matching question encoding for all 768 name-only and 768 wording-versus-both contrasts; six additional parser fixtures passed. No model consumed this revised encoding; it is not a demonstrated learning improvement.

Limits and adoption decision

Both heads failed frozen qualification. Restart replay reproduced the outputs, including wrong answers; repeatability is not correctness. No model or checkpoint was promoted, and the Native NewBrain checkpoint was not retrained by this read-only diagnostic. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. Only reviewed aggregate measurements are published; private records, weights, character references and unlicensed assets remain withheld.
