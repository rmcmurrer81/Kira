Held candidate ranking transfers, but answer abstention still fails

Public aggregate review of committed project results. The website review did not rerun research GPU experiments.

Held transfer — ranking success, qualification failure

Two frozen ranking probes selected the correct candidate on 288/288 supported synthetic held prompts, versus 154/288 for the unchanged conditional rank. Both sides ranked correctly in 128/128 ownership swaps, but after the shared abstention decision only 148/288 supported answers and 102/128 abstentions were correct. Both probes failed qualification; neither was adopted. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

Both probes gained 62 correct supported answers over the support-only comparison without losing any of its supported answers. Each made 26 false copies on unsupported cases. Support-only made 21 false copies and five unresolved ties. Correcting ranking converted those ties into false copies under the shared abstention policy. The added swap penalty did not change any final decision. Two independent CUDA workers reproduced all features and 1,248 continuation scores per run; no training updates occurred during this held transfer.

Separate matched training and calibration

Each ranker completed 120 GPU optimizer updates using the same 216 fit rows. On 104 separate calibration cases, both ranked 72/72 supported questions and both sides of 32/32 swaps correctly; the original rank got both sides of only 1/32 swaps right. With the same support decision, both answered 45/72 supported questions, abstained correctly on 25/32 unsupported cases, and completed 17/32 exact swaps. Cold replay reproduced results; six fixtures passed. These calibration families were distinct from the later 416 held prompts.

Earlier separate held support transfer

On a different 320-case synthetic evaluation, role-aware support gave 120/192 supported exact answers and 112/128 correct abstentions, with 14 false copies and two unresolved ties. The role-masked probe gave 72/192 supported answers and 83/128 abstentions; the original conditional method gave 0/192 and 128/128. None completed both sides of any of the 32 ownership swaps. This separate transfer also failed; it motivated work on rank rather than support alone.

Limits and next checks

These are restricted candidate choices on project-authored synthetic prompts, not unrestricted natural dialogue or evidence of general memory retention. The earlier 1,152 ranker controls, 80 Native drift controls and eight fallback cases remain open for qualification. No checkpoint was adopted. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.
