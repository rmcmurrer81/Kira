What the mechanism review establishes—and does not

The optional readout averages delivered recurrent content-position states instead of using only the terminal state. A separate static source-path audit matched 14 pinned function AST records but did not run a learner or demonstrate causal benefit. Episodic retrieval was excluded from this fixed study; persistent cross-question neural working memory was not established.

The earlier November 2 source-only review said this candidate was unrun at that time. It later ran and failed. A preceding attention-policy study scored 2/6 unsupported-question outcomes on different fresh cases; comparing its score to 0/6 here does not prove regression or improvement.

Cases are consumed, with no tuning or replay. Future comparisons require independently frozen new cases. No accepted new checkpoint, demonstrated Qwen replacement, language generalization or Original12 improvement has been established.
