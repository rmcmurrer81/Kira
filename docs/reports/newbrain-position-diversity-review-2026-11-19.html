Reviewed November 19, 2026 · Completed matched synthetic GPU training · Negative result

NewBrain answer-position training did not improve supported fact selection.

Two independently restored small-model arms each completed 2,048 local GPU optimizer updates, step 20,752→22,800, from the same saved full model and optimizer state. The primary changed the positions of answer-bearing facts; the control retained fixed-front positions. These are synthetic controlled diagnostics, not human-independent general conversation evaluations.

Why the experiment did not qualify

The primary required at least four additional supported pairs over both the original parent and fixed-front control. It scored 26/48: no gain over the parent and three fewer than control. Old bindings, inverse relation and order-transfer guards also regressed. Neither trained model was adopted.

Six local GPU jobs completed, and three independent fresh-process replays reproduced 368 raw answers, including wrong answers, alongside full model/optimizer/random-state checks. Checkpoint storage and readback were verified. These prove execution and repeatability of the recorded outputs, not qualified semantic learning; all 368 synthetic cases are now consumed.

Inverse-render follow-up: prepared, not run

The next comparison has two saved 12,288-row synthetic corpora (6,144 unchanged replay rows and 6,144 modified fact-rendering rows per arm) plus a reserved 384-case evaluation (304 newly prepared complete contexts and 80 consumed controls). Tokenizer, source/input, and admission checks were recorded. Its fixed budget is a planned 2,048 updates per arm, but neither training arm nor its neural inference or cold repeats has run. The proposed wording change also affects token boundaries and answer position, so it is not an isolated mechanism test.

Research boundaries

These internal synthetic trials do not establish reliable generalization, long-term learning, general conversation, voice/vision integration, full-body physiology or humanlike intelligence. Accepted NewBrain Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. Existing live Qwen/person routes and earlier models remain unchanged. This report contains aggregate statistics only—not private source, trained weights, raw prompts, personal information, reference media or unlicensed assets.
