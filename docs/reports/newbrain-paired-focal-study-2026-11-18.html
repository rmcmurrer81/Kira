Reviewed November 18, 2026 · Completed synthetic GPU experiment · Not qualified

A paired NewBrain objective trial shows a narrow gain, with important regressions

Two experimental branches started from the same saved step-16,656 model and optimizer state. Each performed 2,048 local GPU optimizer updates to step 18,704 (4,096 training updates across both arms). Ordinary cross-entropy (CE) served as the matched control; a focal first-answer objective with gamma 2 was the predeclared primary. The data, tokenizer, architecture, sampled training stream and main hyperparameters were held constant.

The focal branch answered 78/128 supported synthetic cases exactly, compared with 73/128 for ordinary CE and 54/128 before either training continuation. This is a 5/128 within-study advantage over CE—not evidence of independent general learning. Only two of seven qualification families passed. No branch was approved for deployment or to replace Qwen. Accepted NewBrain Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

Why the focal result did not qualify

The focal objective passed the 32/32 primary unknown-response gate and 16/16 consumed teaching gate. It failed the supported-binding, complete-group, exact-old-binding, new-wording and secondary-insertion gates: two of seven passed. Compared with ordinary CE, its retained supported answers were 20/32 versus 22/32 and its fresh supported wording was 9/24 versus 11/24. The total 216/320 versus 212/320 includes 23 gains and 19 losses, and does not establish robust transfer. Fourteen primary pairs were wrong in both trained branches.

New complete tables and 16 held-out fact orders were reserved before scoring, but their individual fact bindings had appeared in earlier optimizer inputs; this tests new combinations, not independently new factual labels. Secondary distinct-insertion cases have a lexical-exposure confound. Separate assistant workers authored and reviewed synthetic evaluations; there was no independent human qualification.

What was executed and preserved

The saved records document two completed 2,048-update training streams and separate full model, optimizer, history and random-state checkpoints. Both 320-case cold replays matched byte-for-byte, including incorrect outputs. A distinct saved-artifact audit reported 619,283 checks over training records, plans and checkpoint descriptors. The provisional repository regression suite recorded 112 passed and 35 skipped of 147 tests. These are repeatability and software-integrity observations, not successful language-learning scores. The audit did not independently rerun model inference or training.

Public scope and next work

This is a small experimental local-GPU model, not a trained 9B model or a functioning synthetic companion. No real-person memories, live routing, voice, vision or physical body integration were tested or activated. Private prompts, checkpoints, model files, internal research records, personal data and unapproved media are not published. A future experiment would need to improve supported value binding and genuinely new wording while retaining previous answers and strict unknown-response behavior, on separately frozen tests.
