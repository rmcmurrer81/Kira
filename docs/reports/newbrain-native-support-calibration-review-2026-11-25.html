Separate support weighting experiment — failed qualification

In the separate support-only comparison, equal-weight and extra-weight heads completed 120 CUDA updates each on the same 456 fit cases. Held exact supported answers were 251/320 and 250/320; correct abstentions were 264/288 and 266/288 respectively. Copy logits and ranks were identical between arms. Extra weighting lost three previously correct supported answers, recovered two, and lost one prior abstention while recovering three. It failed both frozen gates. Cold held replay matched exactly with no optimizer updates.

Separate candidate agreement — one recovered abstention

On the separate candidate-agreement bank, the fixed weighted head and fixed copy readout were unchanged. Legacy and agreement rules both answered 157/160 forward and 116/160 reverse supported cases correctly; abstentions were 141/144 forward for both and 137/144 versus 138/144 reverse. The agreement veto corrected one conflicting-owner false copy; zero supported or previously correct abstention losses. The forward abstention improvement gate failed. Both held processes reproduced raw predictions exactly after cold restart, with zero training updates.

Retrospective support-score audit

Retrospective diagnosis authenticated 1,216 saved rows across the two distinct banks. All 640/640 uniquely supported cases had the true candidate's support logit higher than the foil. Of 117 supported misses, 50 had both scores above the cutoff and 67 had both below. A single global cutoff cannot fix both kinds of error. This is an observational audit, not proof of causation or an evaluated new decision rule.

Prospective support-count work — not a behavioral result

A proposed three-class support-count readout distinguishes zero, one or two supported candidates. Fabricated FP32 CUDA features, loss and gradients matched scalar witnesses within 2e-6 tolerance. After preserving two rejected length-admission rounds, all 1,216/1,216 new synthetic prompts passed unchanged token limits (456 fit, 152 calibration, 608 held). No model capture, training, accuracy, restart or retention result exists for this new bank.

The original prompt format exceeded the length cap on 540 rows; a compact revision left six refused rows. A shorter common instruction admitted all rows without truncation or changes to the facts, questions or candidate fields. The exact overlap audit found no matches in the named historical census; it does not establish semantic or training-data independence.

Limits

These are restricted, assistant-authored synthetic candidate tests, not free-form dialogue or proof of neural memory. Earlier 1,152 ranker cases, 80 Native drift cases and eight fallback controls remain unmeasured for these candidates. No checkpoint adopted; accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.
