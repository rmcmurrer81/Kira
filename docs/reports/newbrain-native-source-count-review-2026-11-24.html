Source-count supervision improves abstention but still fails qualification

Public aggregate review of committed summaries and manifests. Research training was not rerun for this website review.

Direct source-count fit — failed

Both disposable readouts completed 128 matched local GPU updates. Warm and cold raw outputs were identical, and all 1,232 earlier baseline outputs were reproduced.

Separate per-record support fit — failed

Both auxiliary arms completed 128 GPU updates. Extra support supervision added zero correct held answers: forward supported 1/64 in both, reverse supported 26/64 versus 25/64, correct abstentions 208/256 in both. Reverse complete swaps declined from 8/32 to 7/32. Both arms assigned highest mass to one source on all 384 held prompts. Exact warm/cold reproduction is evidence of repeatability, not qualification.

Saved-score diagnosis

Offline replay of the direct source-count experiment reproduced 4,352 saved branch decisions over 2,176 consumed cases. Swapping count scores alone reproduced every treated answer, while swapping conditional copy scores alone reproduced every control answer. Count-supervised scores distinguished two sources from zero in 242/256 matched held comparisons; the control tied in all 256. Neither fit reliably identified unique support. This retrospective audit loaded no model and performed no new training.

Factored objective and real tokenizer admission — no model trial yet

A new proposed objective separates unique support from conditional copy and zero-versus-multiple evidence. Twelve fabricated FP32 CUDA comparisons met the frozen numerical tolerance and disposable optimizer restart matched exactly. These were fabricated activations, not model training. A separately frozen benchmark contains 768 synthetic prompts (384 fit and 384 held) with 64 six-condition groups per split. All seven protocol tests passed. All 768 prompts then passed real NewBrain tokenizer admission with 1,232 actual tokenizer calls. An offline reconstruction checked the exact tokens, spans and terminators; held scoring keys were separated. There were zero model forwards, scored model responses or model optimizer updates in the admission stage.

Evidence boundaries

These are distinct assistant-authored synthetic evaluations and consumed-case replays, not human-independent generalization. The factored objective has not been evaluated on learned held-out answering, abstention, retention or multi-turn use. No checkpoint qualified or was adopted.
