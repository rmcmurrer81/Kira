NewBrain · November 22, 2026 · Reviewed separate synthetic trial

A statement-end selector failed its frozen qualification.

Two local matched selector-only training arms were independently verified and tested on frozen assistant-authored synthetic cases. The statement-end arm answered fewer supported questions. No checkpoint qualified for adoption. Accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun.

Two verified CUDA fits, not Native training

Two RTX 5060 Ti workers restored the same step-512 selector, random state, training order and Adam optimizer moments. The control and statement-end selectors each completed 256 FP32 CUDA optimizer updates to step 768 (512 total). Independent readback checked update order and 28 parameter/optimizer storages in each terminal. The terminal states differed; the underlying Native model was not retrained.

This later study is separate from the earlier November 22 three-arm selector comparison. Private checkpoints, prompts and raw receipts remain withheld.

Independent replay and refused preflight

Six isolated held replays recorded 2,304 selector forwards with zero optimizer updates. Later verification reproduced each previously saved token, score and source decision, including errors. An initial replay launch was refused because the trusted Torch setup source was outside its permitted read scope; the corrected scope still excluded held labels.

Earlier wiring checks reproduced one previously exposed calibration copy and admitted 1,152 fit inputs, but those steps are not fresh answer improvements or independent learning evidence.

Boundaries and privacy

These are assistant-authored synthetic cases. Two-answer actual response, natural follow-up, new Native retention replay and general real-world memory remain unproven. No selector adopted; Native unchanged. Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. Only aggregate findings are public, without private research files, checkpoints, personal records, photos or unlicensed media.
