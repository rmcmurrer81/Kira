More copied answers did not fix source ownership.

The isolated factorized selector copied more supported facts but lost important unsupported safeguards, and neither arm followed changing ownership reliably.

Reviewed experiment

A 49,761-parameter rank-32 selector head was trained for 256 local FP32 GPU updates per arm from matched initialization on 2,048 ordered draws of a 1,152-case synthetic fitting inventory. Its cached native features did not retrain the original language model. Calibration was performed on 384 previously consumed synthetic cases: 224 supported and 160 unsupported. This was not a fresh blind held-out test.

The treatment grounded 31/112 forward and 8/112 reverse answers, versus 2/112 and 0/112 control. No setting among 36 treatment margins met all predeclared unsupported floors, including required 28/32 on absent and conflict subgroups. These outcomes fail the acceptance gate.

Wrong ownership sources remain common

The approved exact source ranked first on forward questions in 32/112 control versus 40/112 treatment and on reverse questions in 26/112 control versus 20/112 treatment. Both arms chose the approved source on both sides of just 4/64 assignment swaps. Treatment's largest top-ranked errors were 54/112 reverse questions led by the wrong field type and 44/112 forward questions led by another owner's correct-field candidate. A separate offline role-and-position analysis reused archived calibration scores with zero new neural forwards, tokenization or updates; layout associations are not causal proof.

Separate typed-loss work: source and fabricated CUDA tests only

A proposed typed-ranking loss passed 16/16 fabricated source/protocol checks. Read-only fitting coverage validated 1,152/1,152 cases and 2,048/2,048 scheduled draws. Five fabricated FP32 CUDA cases verified intended gradient targeting and unchanged loss and gradients for unsupported categories; 10/10 fabricated prerequisites passed. There was no real typed-objective fit, no matched trained accuracy comparison and no qualified model-learning result.

No adopted model

The 256-update isolated selector treatment failed its calibration, abstention and source-ranking gates. This is separate from the earlier ranked-binding trial. The fresh diagnostic and original held-out cases were not used to establish an accepted result. Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. No deployed source selector, general learning, stable factual memory or production companion is established.

Evidence boundary

These aggregate counts were reviewed against saved summaries and verification receipts; the private execution was not independently rerun here. Raw synthetic prompts, private software, checkpoints, restricted images, personal media and unlicensed references remain unpublished.
