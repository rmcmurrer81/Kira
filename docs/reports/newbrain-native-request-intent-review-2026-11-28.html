NewBrain uncertainty and request-intent experiments fail qualification

Executed CUDA comparisons — rejected

The November 28 uncertainty-width comparison continued the same step-528 checkpoint for 240 CUDA optimizer updates per arm. Starting, ordinary and uncertainty-weighted endpoints scored 11/48, 8/48 and 8/48 qualified supported answers, and 52/72, 54/72 and 61/72 empty unsupported replies. Uncertain false answers fell from 9/24 to 9/24 and 2/24, while correct uncertain reasons were 0/24, 0/24 and 6/24. Earlier supported recall remained 0/60 and earlier absent accuracy 36/36. Two independent cold workers each reproduced 1,008 raw cases exactly. Both historical vetoes still failed.

A second matched trial continued the weighted step-768 checkpoint for 240 CUDA updates per arm, comparing width-only loss with added request-set ranking. Starting, width-only and ranked endpoints scored 12/48, 8/48 and 7/48 qualified supported answers; 62/72, 63/72 and 63/72 empty unsupported replies; and 27/72, 24/72 and 24/72 correct reasons. Neither fitted endpoint produced a false answer on the 24 uncertain cases, versus 3/24 at start, but both lost supported accuracy. Earlier supported and absent scores remained 0/60 and 36/36. Two independent cold workers each reproduced all 1,008 raw outputs and full state fingerprints. Both historical vetoes still failed.

Saved-output and read-only diagnostics

Saved-output, label-assisted diagnostics implicated descriptive clauses falsely treated as requests and incorrect first/last direction. A read-only equal-token study used two independent 648-forward CUDA processes with zero optimizer updates. Complete intent pairs and complete direction pairs were 0/24 at every endpoint. Correct raw direction on both variants was 10/24, 12/24 and 12/24; this is a component score, not an end-to-end answer. Earlier vetoes remained failed.

Contextual projection and offline comparison

A separate contextual projection measurement used two independent 288-forward CUDA runs and zero optimizer updates. Reconstruction error was within the frozen tolerance (maximum 7.77 × 10⁻⁷). Favorable verb shifts were 0/24, 21/24 and 18/24, yet correct request/description sign crossing was 0/24 at all three endpoints. An offline capped reweighting then compared 288 saved proposals without model forwards or optimizer updates. It changed all 864 clause score vectors but produced zero newly correct qualified answers, zero previous exact losses and zero new false requests; complete intent and direction pairs remained 0/24 at every endpoint. No endpoint earned the required gain.
