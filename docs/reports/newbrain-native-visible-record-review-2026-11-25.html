Cold restart and earlier greedy controls

Separate cold processes reproduced raw outputs, feature and conditional journals, copy ranks and state identities before scoring. Each held process performed 48 GPU forwards and each earlier-memory process 487 greedy forwards; zero optimizer updates. Earlier greedy controls stayed 35/44 exact older supported, 30/36 older unsupported and 13/32 reworded. Of 32 matched older/reworded pairs, older questions scored 23/32; 11 correct answers became incorrect and one incorrect became correct with rewording. The original Native route handled all 112 controls, so this is not evidence of in-scope retention.

Separate fresh sensitivity diagnosis

In a separate fresh 384-case sensitivity test the unchanged checkpoint and support head scored 312/384 exact: 133/192 supported, 179/192 unsupported. All 59 withheld supported answers ranked the correct candidate first and had both support scores below 0.5 (46/96 name-to-object; 13/96 object-to-name). Wording-only changes across 192 matched pairs moved exact answers 149/192 to 163/192 (18 gains, four losses); supported wording alone 60/96 to 73/96 (15 gains, two losses). Record-order changes 105/128 to 103/128 and one irrelevant record replacement 105/128 to 104/128. CUDA cold runs matched byte-for-byte; no weights or thresholds changed.
