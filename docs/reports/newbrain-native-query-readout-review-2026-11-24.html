Matched query readouts recover answers but lose correct abstentions

Public aggregate review of committed experiment summaries. Research training was not rerun for this website review.

Matched query representation fit — failed qualification

Each experimental readout completed 128 matched CUDA updates on the same 640 fit and rehearsal inputs with the frozen decoder and unchanged candidate ordering. The sole representation change was the last question token versus a pooled lookup-term state. Restricting the unchanged ranker to the two eligible candidates was a separate baseline, not a learned improvement.

All eight fallback requests reproduced the unchanged unknown answer.

Saved-score attribution — no new model inference

Neither fitted reader changed the top-ranked candidate on any of 128 supported questions. Every recovered supported answer came from emitting an already-top-ranked candidate. Compared with the restricted original, the pooled reader lost 28 correct held abstentions; all became wrong copies (12 missing source, eight wrong-role mentions, four repeated sources, four conflicts). Replacing the unique-source term alone reproduced 26 of those 28 errors in the saved-score arithmetic. The audit checked 6,144 logged margins; six fabricated tests passed. It performed zero model forwards or optimizer updates and did not regrade answers.

Repeatability and engineering checks

Before training, a read-only CUDA capture measured 40 decoder forwards on 640 fit and rehearsal inputs; independent CPU readback verified the saved feature hashes and all 640 original-ranker records. A fresh CUDA process restored all six fit endpoints. The held evaluation restored both step-128 checkpoints and reproduced raw outputs byte for byte; all 1,232 archived original controls matched, including 80 unchanged Native transcripts. Repeatability does not establish learned recall or generalization.

Evidence boundaries

This is an assistant-authored synthetic ownership benchmark with fresh held wording within a narrow task. One forward fixed-position cell was unmeasured. No general conversation, combinations, or multi-turn learning qualification was established. No model adopted; accepted Original12 remains 2/12, not rerun. No private research files, raw prompts, model weights or private media are released.
