Fact-order training traded one score for another

A completed 2,048-update local CUDA continuation (step 14,608→16,656) diversified synthetic fact-line order. Seven of eight frozen gate families failed. Seen order dropped 32/48→22/48; held-out orders rose 15/48→21/48, driven by improved unknown responses, not improved positive value copying. The experimental checkpoint was preserved, not adopted. Zero-update first-answer diagnostic

Using the fixed 16,656 checkpoint, zero optimizer updates were made. Supported answers scored 39/128, while unsupported/unknown replies scored 30/32. Only 1/16 complete groups passed every reorder/removal control. Old teaching controls scored 16/16; mixed old binding prompts 39/64 (supported 11/32, unknown 28/32). A separate inserted-fact probe scored 19/48 with an exposure/lexical confound.

Sixteen selected consumed teacher-forced traces found correct first answer tokens ranked second through seventh in eight selected supported failures. This identifies wrong selected answer tokens in those traces, not a proven mask bug or fix. Cold replay reproduced 288/288 raw outputs byte-for-byte, wrong answers included. These are diagnostics, not a new learning result.

Separate bounded provenance-history engineering

The metadata layer preserves 2,415 prior provenance records and 96 prior bindings while admitting immutable bounded history shards. The saved sandbox report recorded 147 test identities: 112 passed and 35 skipped (34 optional-dependency skips, one Windows symlink privilege skip; a separate injected guard passed). Configured aggregate limits are 16 immutable shards, 64 MB input and 20,000 records; those are design bounds, not measured full-capacity throughput. These are metadata/software tests, not NewBrainLM training or biological validation.

Evidence boundaries and next stage

All scored language cases are synthetic and consumed. No independent human question set, causal root-cause proof, durable long-term memory, qualified general conversation, speech, vision, physiology, person continuity, 9B trained model or Qwen replacement was established. Software integrity and repeatability do not mean correct reasoning. No private files, source fixtures, checkpoints, synthetic personal histories or character media are reproduced here.
