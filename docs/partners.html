Machine learning

Can NewBrain learn complete answers?

Investigate optimization, token generation, stopping behavior, and retention without adjusting consumed Original12 scoring. Compare carefully matched new experiments with an independently qualified baseline.

Public2 earlier stage: 0/2 → 1/2. A subsequent direct full-answer regimen improved the previously taught pair 1/2 → 2/2 and preserved both answers after fresh-process reload; the curriculum regimen remained 1/2 with less complete-target exposure. Earlier Original12 (64 updates) went 2/12 → 0/12; the distinct 256-update study scored 2/12 → 2/12 after fresh reload, gaining two complete answers while losing both original missing-information passes. A separate Public4 retention pilot ended 2/4 in both arms with no net gain. The newer numerical-gradient diagnostic was inconclusive and its final private execution evidence failed publication.

An earlier read-only diagnostic established the three-token answer was empty at its saved checkpoint; the subsequent direct regimen corrected it on the same previously taught target. Neither result establishes generalization, new-fact learning or a superior curriculum.

Small first collaboration

A proposed 30–90-day pilot.

A limited project, not a promise of results or a request for open-ended laboratory access.

Days 1–30

Reproduce and profile

Agree on a sponsor, access terms, licenses and a bounded protocol; qualify a public control, record baseline CPU/RAM/VRAM/time and retain failed runs.

Days 31–60

Investigate one cause

Run a predeclared learning-versus-generation experiment, preserving original test questions and avoiding post-hoc score repair.

Days 61–90

Review before expanding

Evaluate memory/retrieval or body-event tests with domain specialists. Decide whether a next experiment or hardware allocation is justified.

What would be most useful

First: one faculty sponsor, one student research partner (or approved graduate collaborator), regular mentoring, secure access to shared lab/research computing and a reproducibility checklist.

Later if justified: shared GPU/HPC time, RAM or GPU-laptop loans, approved after-hours access, VR hardware and measured sensor equipment. Current RAM/VRAM/storage targets are planning options—not minimum requirements, and equipment alone cannot solve a learning defect.

Any human-participant or patient-record research would need separate institutional approval, consent, appropriate privacy controls and domain review. Begin with approved public and synthetic data.
