ClearTrail

Keep document revisions, compare exact changes, and prepare briefings beside quoted evidence. The newest source is not automatically approved.

Private prototype

Carry On

Organize work, home or travel plans with tasks, dependencies and readiness. Preserve history and save an export or a restorable workspace copy.

Private prototype

Kira Sequence Desk

Turn supplied notes into a timed, source-linked sequence, associate media, review coverage and export an editable package. It does not render a movie.

The retired experiment organized video plans, references and supplied clips for trimmed, captioned exports. It is preserved as project history; no new generation is offered. A historical submission does not imply a contest result.
