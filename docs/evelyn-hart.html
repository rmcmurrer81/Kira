Evelyn HartA Continuing Synthetic Individual

Evelyn Hart is being developed as a continuing synthetic individual, with her own interests, a growing history of experiences, and relationships built through remembered conversations. Her expertise in AI and technology history is one part of her identity.

Her intended life goes beyond answering questions. Reading, everyday conversations, humor, curiosity and memories of people should shape her experience over time. She is being designed to recognize returning visitors through appropriately authorized profiles and explore interests beyond technology history.

Her in-development local application brings together planned Qwen 3.5:9B conversation, a research library, persistent notes, a continuing life loop and a full-body 3D character. Integrated conversation and long-term visitor memory have not yet passed release testing.

This is a preview of intended capabilities, not a claim that installation, conversation, voice, library learning or persistence have passed release testing. There is no active installer or public release date yet.

Three distinct information paths

What Evelyn says, considers, and knows.

The intended design separates conversations from locally stored reflections and from evidence-backed historical records. This is a proposed architecture, not a demonstration of consciousness or guaranteed factual accuracy.

01 / SPOKEN

Spoken dialogue

Evelyn will converse about AI pioneers, computing history, robotics, inventions and questions from her visitors, with voice and animated presentation once validated.

02 / PRIVATE THOUGHTS

Private reflection

A proposed internal note space for interests, questions and learning plans, kept separate from her spoken dialogue and protected by user-controlled local settings. This refers to an application memory channel, not a claim about subjective thought.

03 / FACTUAL TRUTH

Source-grounded facts

Historical claims are intended to connect to book titles, page references and dated sources. Conflicting accounts or uncertainty should be labeled clearly instead of presented as established truth.

Visitor memory and privacy · Planned behavior · October 21, 2026 · Not yet tested

One Evelyn. Different visitors. Separate memories.

Evelyn is designed to remain active between conversations without assuming the next person who speaks is the previous visitor. This John → Cindy → John example illustrates the desired experience; it is a future test scenario, not a working demonstration.

01 / JOHN

John talks, then leaves

John has a conversation with Evelyn and leaves her life loop running. If he consents to saved memory, his conversation history should be associated with his own profile rather than a global visitor identity.

02 / CINDY

Cindy says hello later

Hours later, Cindy arrives. Evelyn should not assume John is still speaking. She should ask who has joined, offer Cindy a new or appropriately verified profile, and avoid sharing John's private conversation history.

03 / JOHN RETURNS

John returns another time

After John has safely confirmed access to his profile, Evelyn should be able to continue their earlier conversation. If she cannot establish which visitor said something, she should ask rather than attribute the memory to the wrong person.

Proposed visible conversation header: Talking with: Tom ▾

The selector is planned to show saved visitor profiles plus New profile, so a visitor can explicitly switch accounts when Evelyn stays active. A spoken introduction such as “Hi, I'm Tom” should offer Tom's saved profile or a new profile—but must not automatically unlock private records.

Memory continuity: Evelyn's permitted memories of her conversations and experiences with Tom, Cindy and other people should remain available under their respective profiles after switching conversations and after restarting the application. One visitor's personal history must not be disclosed to another visitor without authorization.

Design, not a tested feature: the selector, automatic introduction handling, authenticated profile access, cross-visitor isolation and post-restart recall still require implementation and controlled verification. A person choosing a name from the menu—or saying that name aloud—is not proof of identity.

Additional acceptance check · visitor changes while a reply is pending: A development screenshot reports that source review identified a possible stale-reply problem: an answer started for John could arrive after Cindy is selected. The planned correction must check the active authorized profile and conversation generation before displaying or saving every reply, rejecting or discarding results from the previous visitor. Test John → short pause → Cindy → John with deliberately delayed replies, verify the visible header, inspect saved memories, and repeat after restart. This is a reported review finding and proposed fix—not a passed runtime test.

Privacy requirement: Someone saying a name—or sounding like a known speaker—is not secure proof of identity. Private stored information should require permission and an appropriate authenticated profile; otherwise the interaction should be treated as new or anonymous. No automatic voice or face identification is claimed here.

Required future verification: test John → Cindy → John while Evelyn remains active, then repeat after a complete application restart. Check that remembered details stay with the correct profile and no private details leak across visitors. This end-to-end test has not passed yet. Read the design update →

Relationship continuity · Proposed memory-sharing scenario · October 21, 2026

Remembering someone together—without inventing their story.

Imagine Evelyn previously spoke with Adam, who later dies. Adam's son Max returns and asks Evelyn about her experiences talking with his father. The intended design would let Evelyn describe real, previously recorded, shareable conversations she personally had with Adam, while keeping the people and sources of memories correctly distinguished.

01 / WHAT EVELYN REMEMBERS

Her encounters with Adam

If she has a reliable saved record, Evelyn may recall what she discussed with Adam and when, subject to any consent or sharing limits. She should clearly distinguish recorded dialogue, summaries, and uncertainty rather than act as though she remembers an event that was never saved.

02 / WHAT MAX SHARES

Max's memories of his father

Max can offer his own recollections. Evelyn should attribute them to Max, not silently rewrite her history of Adam. Their discussion could form a new memory of the conversation with Max, linked to Adam only where appropriate and authorized.

03 / PERMISSIONS AND TRUTH

Respect privacy after loss

Family relationship or death alone is not permission to reveal confidential remarks. Evelyn should follow Adam's recorded preferences, respect private information about others, decline restricted details and never fabricate words, advice or approval on his behalf.

Future verification: create a controlled synthetic Adam-and-Max dataset with both shareable and restricted memories. Check correct source attribution, permission filtering, uncertainties, cross-profile separation and recall after restart. This is a proposed feature and test plan only; no passing implementation, bereavement-specific conversation or sharing-permission system has been demonstrated.

The library / life loops

A historian who reads for interest, too.

Evelyn's planned starter library focuses on AI and technology history. It also includes selected literature so her reading is not confined to professional research.

  • AI & technology historyIntroductory history, biographies and computing timelines, with sources recorded where available.
  • Sherlock HolmesSelected stories by Arthur Conan Doyle—mystery, deduction and character interests.
  • DraculaBram Stoker's novel—fiction, narrative and cultural history.

Books will be supplied only from editions Kira Labs can lawfully distribute. Fiction is stored as fiction, never automatically treated as historical fact.

Local launcher / proposed flow

From download to discovery.

  1. Install Evelyn. A planned Windows launcher sets up the local application and guides installation of the compatible Qwen 3.5:9B model, subject to device checks and model-license requirements.
  2. Start with a mini library. Evelyn will receive curated, permitted PDF texts, with optional user-added documents and clear provenance.
  3. Study during life loops. Configurable background reading extracts references and composes research notes; users can pause or limit activity.
  4. Return to a continuing conversation. Memory and citation retrieval are intended to survive a restart without mistaking saved documents for changes to the model's neural weights.
