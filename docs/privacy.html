Plain-language privacy / this website

Your visit.
Your choices.

These notes describe this static Kira Labs website and its optional Iris guide. They are not a privacy policy for every separate Kira Labs product.

Reading the site

The redesigned pages do not add analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or account sign-in. The hosting provider still handles web requests and may process connection information. Follow the host’s privacy documentation for those services.

Existing screenshots and images are site assets. The downloadable preview can request those images from the current public Kira Labs site. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Contacting Robert

The contact form is an email-draft helper. It prepares a mailto link or text you can copy. It does not submit your message to a web server, and it never reports a delivered message. You choose whether to send through your own email service.

Name, reply address and message remain in the open page until you clear or leave it; your browser may apply its own autofill behavior. Once you choose to open or send email, your email software and providers handle it. Do not put passwords, private employee records or other sensitive data in a message.

Asking Iris

Iris matches your question to reviewed public answers in this page’s browser session. No model provider is called, no private Kira World or ShiftBrief service is connected, and the site does not save transcripts in local storage or send them to Robert.

“Delete chat” clears the current conversation and stops spoken playback. Closing or reloading the tab also resets the guide. The guide does not learn from your chat.

Optional spoken replies

“Read reply” uses your browser’s speech-synthesis feature only after you request it. Voice availability and processing depend on your browser, operating system and selected voice; some voices may use an external service. Spoken playback is optional and can be stopped.

Videos, downloads and external services

Public videos are links, not automatically loaded embedded players. Selecting a video, repository, crowdfunding link or product download takes you to that provider. The website guide cannot see what you do in ShiftBrief.

Questions or corrections

Contact rmcmurrer@kiralabs.org. These website notes were prepared September 13, 2026.
