Privacy

Your message.
Your choice.

The contact form sends a message. Standard Iris stays in your tab. Any future enhanced mode requires separate choices for cloud processing and sharing.

Contact messages

When you press Send message, your name, email address, selected reason, and message are transmitted over HTTPS to FormSubmit, which processes the submission for delivery to Robert at rmcmurrer@kiralabs.org. Your email lets Robert reply. Your Iris conversation is not included.

FormSubmit says it retains form submissions for 30 days, including submissions waiting for form-owner activation. Its service may apply spam checks and collect technical information. Read FormSubmit’s privacy terms and its help page. Once delivered, the email is also handled by Robert’s email provider. No specific mailbox retention period is promised here.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card information, sensitive employee records, or confidential files. This form does not accept attachments. Contact Robert for corrections or deletion requests.

The form uses a normal browser submission. FormSubmit may show a spam check or confirmation page. In a downloaded review this opens in another tab; on the published website it can return to Kira Labs. The receiver must activate the form before delivery is enabled.

Iris and published knowledge

Iris loads versioned public knowledge files and uses the original website guide’s answer and follow-up logic in your browser. A name change does not grant access to any private record. The guide covers published biography, screen credits, books, and project notes, with the current website’s project selection applied.

In standard mode, your questions and replies stay in this tab’s memory. There are no model-provider calls, saved transcripts, microphone requests, or automatic forwarding to the contact form. New chat clears the standard conversation. Original public assets may be downloaded from Kira Labs or its public GitHub repository; those requests retrieve published files, not your questions.

Optional voice playback uses your browser’s speech synthesis. Available voices and any network processing depend on your browser and selected voice.

Hosting and links

GitHub Pages hosts the public website. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged for security. External services such as FormSubmit, GitHub, IMDb, Amazon, Facebook, and YouTube have their own privacy practices. No new site analytics or advertising trackers are included in this revision.

Video previews

Video cards request thumbnails from YouTube’s image service. Opening a video on YouTube takes you to that service. On a hosted version of the website, choosing Play may load YouTube’s embedded player. The downloaded review opens the watch page instead. No video player loads automatically, and video playback is separate from Iris and the contact form.

Optional enhanced Iris — not enabled by default

The current standard guide remains available without hosted AI. The enhanced feature is disabled until its private service is configured and tested. When available, its controls appear beside Iris. Turning on enhanced matching sends your question and up to four recent exchanges to Cloudflare for model-assisted selection of reviewed public notes. Model output cannot add facts to the answer bank.

Sharing with Robert is a separate choice

The Share future questions and replies checkbox is off by default. Only when selected are the new question, Iris’s reply, source references, topic, time and optional helpful/not-helpful vote saved in a private database for up to 30 days. Past conversation is not automatically uploaded for storage. A random session reference distinguishes sessions, not unique people. Network addresses are not stored in conversation records; short-lived keyed rate-limit identifiers are retained separately for abuse controls. Turnstile and hosting providers may process network information under their own policies.

Robert’s weekly digest covers only these opted-in records. It is delivered through Resend to rmcmurrer@kiralabs.org and may contain excerpts of questions and answers. Common email addresses, phone numbers and credential patterns are removed where detected, but this is not guaranteed anonymization. Do not submit passwords, confidential documents, private employee data or sensitive personal information to the chat.

Remembering and deleting

Remember this conversation stores up to 20 exchanges and the session deletion key on your device for 30 days. It is optional and should not be used on a shared device. Delete saved conversation removes the device copy and the current session’s shared server records. If deletion cannot be confirmed, the key is kept so you can retry. Turning sharing off stops future storage; it does not silently erase records already shared. New chat starts a fresh discussion but is not a server-deletion command. Without device memory, the deletion key is lost when the tab closes; server records still expire. Contact Robert for help.

Deletion cannot recall emails already delivered to Robert. D1’s provider-managed recovery copies can remain for the free plan’s recovery window after deletion; emailed records follow mailbox retention. Iris never merges one visitor’s conversation into another’s. Proposed knowledge additions require Robert’s approval and a public source, and can be rolled back.
