The opportunity

A lab positioned between local AI, creator tools and persistent digital people.

Kira Labs is building toward software that lets AI people, creative workflows and virtual places stay connected over time. The long-term vision includes Kira World, synthetic people with separate identities and memories, local creative production tools, avatar systems, and controlled cognitive-architecture research.

The business opportunity is not one product yet. It is a founder-led platform direction with several possible commercial paths that need validation: creator tools, paid pilots, licensing, technical demonstrations, educational experiences, digital-continuity workflows and immersive environments.

What exists today

Evidence, not hype.

Kira Labs separates completed checks, working prototypes, active development and future concepts. Negative and tied results remain visible because credibility matters more than a forced success story.

01

Video Studio

Local creative-video workflow work exists, including an actual short MP4 evidence clip with sound. Longer-film orchestration and complete 360° production remain unfinished.

02

NewBrain · first Kira Labs model

NewBrain is Kira Labs’ first internally developed experimental trainable cognitive model. On one exposed command-learning task, rehearsal retained 216/216 earlier and 162/162 new examples in each of five runs. General conversation, broad sensory understanding and superiority to Qwen remain unproved.

03

Kira World

The broader world direction combines identity, memory, conversation, digital places and future embodiment. Current systems do not claim consciousness or a finished virtual life.

04

Public narrative

The public website, research pages, business page and YouTube updates make the project inspectable and easier to discuss with partners, sponsors and future investors.

Business model hypotheses

Several paths, each still to be tested.

Creator software

Subscription or paid desktop tools for local video production, project memory, revisions and source-aware workflows.

Paid pilots and demonstrations

Scoped partner pilots around hardware, VR, video workflows, avatar demonstrations or research benchmarks.

Licensing and integrations

Future licensing of selected workflow, memory, avatar or world-building components if they become reliable enough for other developers or organizations.

Digital-continuity and immersive experiences

Longer-term products around persistent synthetic people, approved memories, virtual places, educational companions and continuity experiences.

What capital should unlock.

These are intended milestones, not promises. The point of investor capital would be to make progress measurable and easier to review.

  1. Video Studio proof

    Move from short verified output toward a repeatable longer-form workflow with preserved sources, narration, edits and reviewable project state.

  2. NewBrain model milestones

    Extend beyond the current task-specific retention result into qualified dialogue, restart-persistent memory, sound and vision learning, matched controls and fair local Qwen comparisons with resource measurements.

  3. Avatar and world demonstration

    Produce a bounded Kira World demonstration that distinguishes actual current capability from concept art and future embodiment goals.

  4. Public pilot package

    Package one partner-facing demonstration, one investor update, and one public video/report that clearly states what worked, what failed and what comes next.

Risks and boundaries

Clear limits make the company more investable, not less.

Kira Labs is early, pre-revenue and founder-dependent. Several projects are prototypes or research plans rather than finished products. NewBrain has not demonstrated general conversational parity or superiority to Qwen. Avatar Builder is not a finished body system. Kira World does not claim consciousness. Healthspan Lab is not medical advice or a clinical product.

The investment case depends on converting this body of work into focused, usable products and paid pilots. That is exactly why the first outside support should be tied to measurable milestones.
