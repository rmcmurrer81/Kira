Known visible and dialogue problems: the hair and scalp separate during some head turns; some replies say an action will be done later even while the video shows it happening, and phrases such as “queued commands” sound overly formal. Limits: the interviewer prompts and movement controls were prepared beforehand. Read the recorded dialogue transcript

Interviewer: Hi Evelyn. Introduce yourself briefly, then turn your head to your left and look left.

Evelyn: Hello! I'm Evelyn, a historian of AI and old tech. I'll turn my head left for you now.

Interviewer: What interests you about old computers? Turn right, then look up.

Evelyn: I find their physical design and the stories behind them fascinating. I will follow your queued commands to look right and up later.

Interviewer: Look down, keep your head still, and move just your eyes left and right.

Evelyn: I will execute those eye movements for you shortly.

Interviewer: Close your eyes for a moment, open them, blink, and look back at me. What would you like to read next?

Evelyn: I will close my eyes, open them, and blink for you shortly. Next, I would like to read about early attempts at automating calculations, perhaps exploring the history of mechanical design.

Evelyn Hart · October 22, 2026 · Planned tests, not accepted movement

She should move like a person.
Not remain stiff in a T-pose.

The intended first complete female body must work in Evelyn’s own app. Her initial room needs only a chair, table and library tablet, with natural posture and coordinated movement rather than a static render.

01 / BODY

Complete female anatomy first

Preserve the verified reference asset. Demonstrate creation of a complete editable female body before trying picture/video-guided variations on additional characters.

02 / MOVEMENT

Relax, sit, reach and read

Test weight shifts, smooth transitions, sitting, posture adjustments, reaching for the tablet, coordinated face, eye and head movement. Record head close-ups during actual speech and blinking tests.

03 / CLOTHING

Repeat the recorded motions

Once body movements pass independently, fit realistic clothing and compare the same moves for natural drape, folds, fabric collision and clipping. Her private owner installation and conversations precede any public release.

Next room-and-movement study · Proposed after voice qualification

A comfortable recliner, a table and a library tablet.

Evelyn's proposed room will use a realistic padded reclining chair with a supportive headrest, armrests and footrest, plus a proportionally sized table and reachable tablet. The setup should allow repeatable studies of approaching the chair, sitting, relaxing, reclining, reaching to read, and standing without clipping or abrupt movements.

Not yet measured: the furniture's final 3D geometry, comfortable appearance, coordinated reach mechanics, collision clearance, integrated speech and finished movement footage. These are planned test objects, not completed scene assets or proven behavior.

Still unverified: automatic photo/video body creation, complete humanlike full-body animation, fabric behavior, tablet interaction, voice synchronized with movement, and a release-ready installer. The saved censored three-view reference is not a generated personalized variant. Private personal memories remain separate from a future downloadable version.

Evelyn · October 23, 2026 · Adult-avatar publication safeguards · Source progress

Public previews without exposing her private anatomy.
Selective coverage is now in source review, not yet in verified footage.

Kira Labs is preparing a two-version workflow for future authorized adult-avatar body captures: preserve the private uncensored original for the owner's university review, and prepare a separate censored public image, video or thumbnail for review before posting. The newer selective-cover code is designed to follow the rendered body and camera rather than obscuring an entire frame. This is not yet an approved moving-video privacy result.

01 / SELECTIVE COVERS

Intended public display

The earlier privacy fallback hid the entire frame in black. Current implementation work instead positions opaque coverage against the current body and camera view, so future status updates can retain a useful silhouette while hiding private anatomical regions. Static code review is not proof of safe all-frame coverage.

02 / ORIGINALS STAY PRIVATE

Separate files and thumbnails

Private originals and public candidates remain distinct, including thumbnail paths. Public derivatives must be visually inspected before they are supplied for website or social posting. Two picture-metadata integration defects have source corrections; runtime verification of metadata removal, frame encoding and privacy remains outstanding.

03 / CLEANUP GATE

Never lose a private recording

A newly found stream-finalization issue could interfere with preserving the private recording if a public copy fails. A narrow correction has been written and is still under review. Recording and cleanup must pass end-to-end before an anatomy video can be presented as real progress.

Public evidence boundary: no new qualified adult body screenshot, movement video, anatomy acceptance or original voice recording is available from this source work.
