NewBrain · October 19, 2026

256 training updates reported; strict final Original12 score pending

Training report · final grading pending

The October 19 university briefing reports completion of four 64-update blocks (256 updates, 1,536 repeated presentations) and a fresh-process generation from the saved trained state without additional updates. A released paired Original12 accuracy score is not yet available.

The latest independently internally reviewed evidence committed to GitHub covers the first three blocks: 192 updates, 1,152 presentations and authenticated state lineage. The fourth block and final generation are described in the newer briefing, but their saved evidence is not yet reflected in that committed research record. Training completion and generated output files do not establish better learned answers.

All four current launch controls—normal completion (B0), parent loss (B1), revised cold-library startup (L0) and worker/provider control (W0)—qualified in scoped internal reviews. The earlier failed L0 and an initial failed W0 audit remain preserved; corrected fresh testing or narrowly reviewed checks qualified the later controls.

What has not yet changed

The original Original12 baseline was 2/12, with two missing-information responses; an earlier separate trained/reloaded study scored 0/12. Public2's 2/2 fresh-reload result concerns two previously taught examples, not this Original12 run. Qwen, held-out learning and general conversation remain untested. The earlier 512-vertex skin-geometry result is not physiological skin. A reviewed avatar-loader correction and 13-check harness are not yet applied or runtime-tested; clinical lung/nerve integration remains unvalidated.

NewBrain / targeted trained-answer persistence · 2026-10-18

NewBrain produces and reloads a complete three-token answer

2/2 correct on two previously taught public answers

After an earlier Public2 experiment learned the short answer but stopped immediately on the longer one, a separate direct full-answer training regimen corrected cup word one and preserved both exact answers after a distinct fresh-process model reload.

Both arms began from the same pinned, previously taught 1/2 checkpoint. Each used 64 updates and 128 repeated presentations. The direct regimen reached 2/2 after training and 2/2 after restart. The curriculum regimen stayed 1/2 and generated the incomplete cup word c. Its longer-answer exposure was not equal to the direct arm: 32 versus 64 full-target presentations and 336 versus 384 token targets including EOS. Thus the comparison does not isolate ordering or demonstrate that direct training is universally better.

An independent internal saved-evidence review checked the original results and a separate worker's four saved-state generations without extra optimization. The fresh worker reproduced all four saved training outputs. The earlier 0/2 → 1/2 Public2 experiment and separately failed Original12 2/12 → 0/12 experiment remain historical results; neither was retroactively rescored.

Limits of this result

Previously taught public examples, not novel facts, held-out generalization, delayed long-term retention, interference resistance, general conversation or a tested Maya brain. Short-answer rehearsal means interference resistance cannot be inferred. Independent internal review does not replace external university replication.

NewBrain / diagnostic · 2026-10-18

A read-only diagnostic checks loss accounting and confirms early stopping

Internally audited numerical and generation measurements

Four execution controls qualified. Three objective calls returned a short-row loss of 1.3116488, a longer-row loss of 2.4736416 and a combined batch loss of 2.0863106.

The combined loss matched the target-count-weighted two row losses within 1e-12, ruling out a simple reduction mismatch for this fixed checkpoint and batch. With zero updates and no new scoring, the short saved output was ball; the three-token target cup word one still generated an empty response and immediate end-of-sequence. Per-position logits, EOS competition and the cause of early stopping remain unknown.

Evidence boundary

Independent internal saved-data review is not external replication or a model-wide theorem. Original12 remains 2/12 baseline versus 0/12 after separate training; trained-on Public2 remains 0/2 → 1/2. The next targeted acquisition/stopping study is only proposed.

NewBrain / numerical methods · 2026-10-18

Thirty sampled gradients independently checked

Bounded internal numerical verification · not a learning gain

A saved-data reviewer verified 30 sampled coordinates at each of three step sizes—90 central-difference comparisons from 181 original objective calls—across ten arrays containing 11,914 model parameters.

This verifies the chosen local derivative sample at one fixed Public2 checkpoint and batch. It does not establish that every derivative is correct, reveal the cause of the Original12 regression, or show better full-answer accuracy. Prior failed audit attempts were preserved. A separate objective-versus-generation diagnostic has qualified its normal-execution and parent-exit controls; its remaining cold-library and worker-limit controls and its actual scientific run are still pending in the dated record.

What this means

Independent internal saved-evidence review is not independent university replication. Original12 stays at 2/12 before training and 0/12 after separate training; Public2 stays 0/2 to 1/2 after training and reload. Neither figure should be combined with the gradient count.

Kira World / Companion robotics · 2026-10-16

A bounded ROS 2 bridge for a future companion robot

Standalone reference tested · integration planned

Kira Labs now explains how a continuing Kira World identity could eventually request safe speech, gaze, expression and gesture behaviors from a future companion robot—without putting private memory or direct motor commands inside a generic bridge.

The existing ROS 2 intention-bridge prototype contains message definitions, bounded safety admission, mock session lifecycle logic and hash-linked evidence. A project validation report dated August 18 records 88 passing ROS-independent tests, four valid example intentions, one intentional policy rejection and 18 checked mock lifecycle events. These are local software checks, not robot movements.

The longer-term research proposal would let Kira World maintain each individual's distinct identity and memories, while a future NewBrain component might provide measured learning or decision inputs. An approved simulator or robot would retain physical execution authority, and its acceptance, rejection, completion or interruption status would return to Kira World. The proposed avatar-to-robot transition and return have not been implemented.

What this means

Not yet connected: no authenticated live Kira World session, connected NewBrain output, built-and-validated target ROS 2 simulator adapter, or physical companion robot has passed an end-to-end test. A new architecture diagram is illustrative rather than runtime evidence.

Kira World / Patient Horizon · 2026-10-16

Planning a physiology-to-avatar bridge

Architecture proposal · not yet integrated

Kira Labs proposes combining Pulse for simulated physiology, Three.js for visible anatomy, and a local interface to NewBrain. This is a design step, not a running three-system demonstration.

The plan keeps each role distinct: Blender and Avatar Builder would provide approved body geometry, Pulse is a candidate for respiratory and circulatory calculations, Three.js would visualize modeled state, and a versioned local bridge would carry timestamped units, provenance, per-avatar identity and errors. NewBrain would need its own measured output and input tests; the missing neural-to-respiratory-muscle route is not supplied automatically by Pulse.

Original Kira Labs illustrations accompany the research explanation. They depict possible medical-style screens and an anatomical avatar; neither is a screenshot or a patient-specific medical result.

What this means

No Pulse–Three.js–NewBrain integration has passed a test. The separately verified 512-vertex forearm geometry result does not establish breathing, gas exchange, touch or physiological feedback. The current NewBrain learning score remains 2/12.

Lung–body–NewBrain research requirements documented

Active research · requirements only

KiraWorld documents a future two-way lung, breathing-muscle, circulation and NewBrain pathway. A breathing animation alone is not functioning physiology.

The requested work separates neural control from chest and diaphragm motion, air exchange, transport of gases through blood and circulation, and measured sensory feedback to a genuine NewBrain receiver. A separate body experiment moved and exactly restored 512 measured weighted forearm vertices under a five-state control. That geometric result is not evidence of a working lung.

What this means

NewBrain · 2026-09-29

Controlled NewBrain learning research begins

Active development

NewBrain moves from cell-model and provenance work into a controlled learning comparison designed to decide whether a neural-like component earns a larger role.

The current evaluator compares six approaches, including simple indexed memory, using the same teaching events and memory limit. It measures learning, retention, reversal learning, interference and false associations. The small prototype has 32 adjustable weights, and a tie with simple memory is treated as no demonstrated advantage rather than a success.

Earlier work independently checked a restricted GLIF1 human-cell model with Brian2 and reproduced an engineered synthetic association pattern. Those checks establish useful software and simulator evidence; the learning rule remains synthetic and is not presented as validated human plasticity.

What this means

NewBrain is early research, not a recreated human brain or a consciousness claim. Kira’s live identity, reviewed memories and public speech are disconnected from the benchmark. If results justify further work, the first Kira World connection is intended to be a restricted, reversible sidecar rather than an immediate replacement of the existing architecture.

About the date: repository and development records from September 29, reviewed September 30, 2026. The learning benchmark was still in progress at review time.
