NewBrain · 2026-09-29

Controlled NewBrain learning research begins

Active development

NewBrain moves from cell-model and provenance work into a controlled learning comparison designed to decide whether a neural-like component earns a larger role.

The current evaluator compares six approaches, including simple indexed memory, using the same teaching events and memory limit. It measures learning, retention, reversal learning, interference and false associations. The small prototype has 32 adjustable weights, and a tie with simple memory is treated as no demonstrated advantage rather than a success.

What this means

NewBrain is early research, not a recreated human brain or a consciousness claim. Kira’s live identity, reviewed memories and public speech are disconnected from the benchmark.
