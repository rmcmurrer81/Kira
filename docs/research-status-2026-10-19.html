Scientific limits and collaboration

Keep prior results
in their own experiments.

The preserved Original12 baseline scored 2/12, with two missing-information responses rather than learned facts. An earlier separate 64-update trained/reloaded study scored 0/12. The newly completed 256-update study independently recorded 2/12 before and 2/12 after reload, gaining two full answers while losing both original missing-information passes. All three saved states remain separate.

Public2's successful direct regimen scored 2/2 on two previously taught public answers after fresh reload; this is not unfamiliar-fact acquisition. Qwen remains untested. The avatar separately moved and exactly restored 512 weighted vertices across five commanded poses, but realistic passive skin, biological nerves, functional lungs and medical simulation remain unvalidated. An avatar-loader correction and 13-check harness have passed source review but remain unapplied and runtime-untested.

University collaboration priorities: strict independent scoring and replication, sequence-generation analysis, reproducible learning experiments, biomechanical and physiological validation, and controlled compute resources. Extra hardware may enable experiments, but does not guarantee better learning accuracy.
