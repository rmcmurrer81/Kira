About Kira Labs

Evidence first.
A more capable and humane future.

Kira Labs is an independent AI research initiative based in Newark, New Jersey. We explore cognitive architectures, persistent synthetic identities, accessible technology and future medical and robotic applications—while documenting what works and what still needs testing.
