Public2 / new direct full-answer regimen

1/2 → 2/2

Correct three-token answer survives reload

Starting from a previously taught public checkpoint, 64 direct full-answer updates and 128 repeated presentations corrected the complete cup word one target. A distinct fresh worker restored the saved state and reproduced both exact outputs.

Internally independently audited. Previously exposed answers, not new facts, unseen questions or delayed retention. The different curriculum regimen remained 1/2.

NewBrain / trained-on Public2

0/2 → 1/2

One answer correct after reload

After 64 updates and 128 repeated presentations, the saved model produced ball correctly after fresh-process reload. The three-token answer ended immediately without a word.

Not evidence of held-out generalization, delayed retention or a useful conversational model.

NewBrain / separate Original12

2/12 → 0/12

A documented regression

Two baseline passes were missing-information responses rather than acquired facts. After a separate 64-update, 384-presentation training run, no complete answers passed.

The preserved 2/12 baseline and failed 0/12 trained snapshot are distinct states. The cause remains unproven.

What a collaborator could test next

Latest Public2 evidence: the direct full-answer regimen corrected the previously failing long trained-on answer and retained it after a fresh-process reload. A curriculum regimen with less full-answer exposure did not. The next experiment should equalize answer-token exposure and add genuinely held-out tests before claiming an ordering advantage.

After the completed read-only objective check, investigate per-position token logits, end-of-sequence competition, answer-length behavior and a newly predeclared learning/retention experiment. Qualify any body-event input path separately. Any improved result must come from fresh, prespecified scoring rather than replaying the consumed Original12 run.
