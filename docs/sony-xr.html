Exploratory collaboration concept

A modern magic mirror — with someone you can talk to.

Imagine looking into a display and seeing a familiar digital person: an expressive 3D presence that listens, speaks and, with permission, remembers past conversations. Not just an animation or a one-time chatbot. This is the experience Kira Labs would like to explore with Sony's glasses-free Spatial Reality Display.

What might a visitor experience?

"Hello again. Want to continue where we left off?"

A fictional guide, storyteller, historical expert or personal companion appears within a spatial display, responds through voice and uses explicitly approved continuity. The scene is a concept to be built and tested, not footage of an existing integration.

Prototype first. Measure everything. Scale only after proof.

01 / What exists today

  • Kira World: separate person profiles, reviewed continuity/memory, local text and conditional voice routes, plus an early 3D World Shell.
  • Avatar Builder: body rigging and controlled movement work; a production-ready fully animated resident body remains unverified.
  • NewBrain: an independent experimental trainable cognitive architecture studying plasticity, retention and sensory learning. It is not currently a general chatbot replacement or integrated avatar controller.

02 / Why Sony

The Sony ELF-SR2 is a 27-inch glasses-free Spatial Reality Display with viewer tracking and developer resources. It offers a compelling way to explore a visible conversational character without requiring every viewer to wear a headset.

The feasibility of an actual Kira World-to-display application, SDK path, content requirements, licensing and host-PC performance remains to be confirmed with Sony's team.

04 / Smallest credible pilot

  1. Review Sony SDK, software stack, licenses and test hardware with the XR team.
  2. Present one fictional resident, one stationary 3D scene, and an explicitly approved memory example.
  3. Connect text/voice turns to mouth, gaze and gesture cues with user control.
  4. Measure frame rate, response latency, RAM/VRAM, local privacy, restart recall and failure behavior.

05 / Hardware and collaboration

Documented local development platform: Intel Core Ultra 9 285K, 32 GB DDR5, RTX 5060 Ti (16 GB), 2 TB SSD (September 2026 snapshot; upgrades subject to confirmation).

Request: brief XR engineering conversation; development guidance; access to a demonstration or evaluation display; information on developer, test, loan or sponsorship opportunities.

Larger RAM, GPU VRAM and Gen5 storage are research expansion options—not claims about Sony's SDK minimum requirements.

07 / Possible use cases to evaluate

  • Interactive fictional or historical guides and museum characters.
  • A familiar digital companion that continues conversations across visits.
  • Educational displays with a character who can answer questions.
  • Future home, desk or public installations—subject to privacy, accessibility and safety testing.

These are concepts, not released products or established health outcomes.
