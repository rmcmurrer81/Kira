# Kira Safe Start Navigator

**Hackathon:** Open Atlas — AI for Social Good  
**Mode:** Solo concept  
**Priority:** Low because finalists must attend live judging in Milpitas, California, at their own expense.

## Concept

A source-backed arrival and stabilization checklist for someone moving to a new city after housing loss, domestic violence, disability, immigration, disaster, or another major transition.

## Solo MVP

- User selects a synthetic scenario and destination
- Agent builds a checklist for identification, housing, transit, benefits, healthcare access, accessibility, and local support
- Every recommendation keeps its source, date, and uncertainty status
- No real sensitive personal data is used in the demo

## Boundary

Do not present this as legal, medical, immigration, or benefits eligibility advice. It is an organizational and source-navigation prototype.
