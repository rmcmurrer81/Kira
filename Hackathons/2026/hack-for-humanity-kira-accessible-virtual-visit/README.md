# Kira Accessible Virtual Visit

**Hackathon:** Hack for Humanity — AI for Mental and Physical Health  
**Mode:** Solo concept  
**Current status:** Planning only until the organizer clarifies whether AI-assisted coding counts as prohibited outside assistance.

## Concept

A browser-based, low-motion virtual outing for people who cannot easily travel because of hospitalization, fatigue, disability, recovery, or limited mobility.

## Solo MVP

- One original virtual location, such as a quiet observatory or garden
- Seated navigation
- No-motion, slow-motion, and guided modes
- Keyboard operation and large controls
- Captions, high contrast, and adjustable text size
- Pause, stop, continue, or remain-quiet choices
- A simple conversational guide using only synthetic demonstration data

## Safety and claims

This is an accessible recreational and social experience. It must not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, or rehabilitate any medical condition.
