# Under-three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“Small businesses lose continuity because decisions, deadlines, risks, and customer signals are scattered across notes and conversations. Large companies have operations teams. Solo founders often do not.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Product

“This is Kira FounderOps, a Gemini-powered continuity agent for small businesses. It turns messy founder input into an evidence-grounded operating brief, and it requires human approval before the plan is accepted.”

## 0:40–1:05 — Show the inputs

- Open the app.
- Click **Load Kira Labs sample**.
- Briefly show the objective, notes, known metrics, and honest statement of `$0` revenue/no paying customers.

Say: “The prompt prohibits invented customers, revenue, metrics, and commitments.”

## 1:05–1:45 — Run live Gemini

- Click **Run Gemini operations agent**.
- Do not cut away while the request is running.
- Show executive summary, decisions and their evidence, risks, next actions, outreach draft, and unknowns.

Say: “Every brief is a live Gemini API call. Structured output makes the AI part of an operating workflow instead of a chat window.”

## 1:45–2:10 — Show AI execution evidence

- Point to the model badge and storage status.
- Open `data/agent_runs.jsonl`, a Firestore document, or Cloud Run logs.
- Show model version, timestamp, response ID, and token usage.

Say: “The application logs what AI did so the work can be reviewed.”

## 2:10–2:35 — Human gate

- Click **Human approve action plan**.
- Show the approval timestamp and `approved_actions.jsonl` or Firestore approval record.

Say: “The AI analyzes and drafts. The human verifies and approves. The prototype does not automatically email, spend, or change outside systems.”

## 2:35–2:55 — Business and impact

“Kira FounderOps begins as an internal Kira Labs tool and is designed for solo founders and small teams. A future subscription can help businesses preserve decisions, reduce founder overload, and create opportunities for onboarding, integrations, support, and industry-specific templates.”

## 2:55–3:00 — Close

“Kira FounderOps: continuity for the businesses that cannot afford an operations department.”

## Recording notes

- Keep the final video under 3:00.
- Use only copyright-safe audio, or no music.
- Upload publicly to YouTube or Vimeo and test the link in a private browser window.
- Do not claim Cloud Run, Firestore, customers, or revenue unless you actually demonstrate/provide them.
